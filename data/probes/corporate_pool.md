# Corporate board candidate-pool probe

Ten corporate frame companies were checked against official annual reports/AGM notices for 2019–2025:

ASML, AstraZeneca, Inditex, L'Oréal, LVMH, Nestlé, Novartis, Roche, SAP, Siemens.

## Extracted

- 420 company-year rows.
- Board openings, departures, new members, CEO changes, nomination-committee references.
- Candidate-list publication status for each company-year.

## Result

Corporate boards publish **seat changes and appointee names**, but not candidate pools or applicant counts. `corporate_candidate_list` rows therefore record whether a list was published; no candidate denominator is available.

This means corporate-board overproduction cannot be estimated from public company reporting. Board turnover can be measured; candidate competition cannot.
