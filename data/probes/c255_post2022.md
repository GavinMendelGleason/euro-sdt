# Article 255 TFEU panel — candidate pool, 2023–2025

Accessed 2026-11-03. Scope: annual counts of candidates assessed by the panel provided for by Article 255 TFEU for 2023, 2024 and 2025, split between first-term and renewal candidates.

## Bottom line

**No annual 2023/2024/2025 counts are public.** The panel's eighth activity report — the only document that carries its per-year table — is listed on the official committee site as **"(In progress)"** with no English or French file. The last published report (seventh, 14 July 2022) covers the third panel only and stops at 28 February 2022.

What is public is the **cumulative** series to the end of the fourth panel's mandate (28 February 2026), from the committee's Work page. It is not annual, but it bounds the 2023–2025 window exactly.

Two consequences, both arithmetic on the same official series rather than new published figures:

- Fourth mandate renewals = 150 − 113 = **37**.
- Fourth mandate first-term opinions = 163 − 130 = **33**.
- Fourth mandate unfavourable = 36 − 28 = **8**.

The subtrahends (113 renewals, 130 first terms, 28 unfavourable, 243 opinions) are the seventh report's cumulative totals to 28 February 2022 (https://op.europa.eu/documents/5642886/10597137/2022.2597-QCAR22002ENN_002.pdf). They are marked `partial` in the CSV and must not be presented as published annual figures.

## Why 2023–2025 are blank

1. **Eighth report not published.** The Work page carries the entry "## (In progress) Eighth Activity report / Available in: FR EN" with `href="#"` for both languages. There is no PDF link, unlike reports 1–7. The site's own cumulative statement is dated to "the end of the term of office of the fourth panel, on 28 February 2026", so the report period is 1 March 2022 – 28 February 2026; 2023–2025 sit inside it but are not broken out.
2. **No interim annual report exists.** The Work page lists only reports 1–7 (2011, 2013, 2013, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2022). No 2023, 2024 or 2025 report.
3. **FOI confirms no aggregate table is released.** Council note 12964/26 of 10 September 2026 (confirmatory application 48/c/01/26; https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-12964-2026-INIT/en/pdf) records a request for "the list of votes and opinions … for the period 1 March 2022 to 28 February 2026". The Council replied that "the only documents … identified as falling in the scope of your request are the opinions issued by the panel" — i.e. no list, table or register — and granted only "a redacted version of seven opinions … by way of a sample". The applicant's confirmatory application notes that "the latest published report is the Seventh Activity Report (14 July 2022)".
4. **Earlier FOI attempts also refused.** AskTheEU requests 24/2477 (confirmatory refusal 9 January 2025; https://www.asktheeu.org/request/opinions_of_the_255_committee_on) and 25/0444 (refused 31 March 2025, confirmatory reply 5 June 2025; https://www.asktheeu.org/request/selection_of_candidates_for_the) sought the panel's opinions for 2023–2024 and for Portuguese candidates; both were refused on privacy, decision-making and commercial-interest grounds. Neither reply states candidate or opinion counts for a calendar year.

5. **Council/IGC working documents carry no annual count.** Documents examined: ST 8152/25 (confirmatory application 11/c/01/25), ST 12964/26 (48/c/01/26), ST 15907/25 (appointment of the fifth panel), ST 11681/23 and ST 13841/24 (appointment decisions), ST 13358/24, ST 13680/24 and ST 14108/24 Rev 1 (CRGMS notes on the 2024 Court of Justice and General Court appointments). All record individual opinions or appointments; none contains a per-year table of candidates assessed.

## Candidate-pool vs other categories

The 313/150/163 series counts **opinions delivered** — generally one per candidate assessed, including re-submissions after an unfavourable opinion. It is not:
- **openings** (seats filled) — appointment decisions in the Conference of Representatives (e.g. ST 11681/23, ST 13841/24) record only successful candidates;
- **applicants** — national selection files are held by Member States, not the Council;
- **formal nominees** — each government forwards a single name, but the panel also sees re-submissions after an unfavourable opinion, and those re-submissions are inside the 70.

Do not add these to the 163.

## Context figures (not annual, not candidate counts)

- Panel president Allan Rosas, Strasbourg seminar, 27 November 2025: "the average rate of negative opinions on candidates for a first term of office has been around 20 to 25 %" over 2010–2025. Consistent with the 22% cumulative figure; gives no annual split.
- Fifth panel appointed by Council Decision (EU) 2025/2644 of 16 December 2025, term 1 March 2026 – 28 February 2030 (ST 15907/25; committee Composition page). The fourth panel's mandate therefore ended 28 February 2026 and the eighth report is its final report.
- Seventh activity report's last annual row: 2022 covers only 1 January – 28 February 2022 and records 10 opinions (CJ: 1 renewal, 1 first term; GC: 8 renewals, 1 first term). This is the boundary year, not part of the 2023–2025 window.

## What failed / could not be used

- **Council public register** (`consilium.europa.eu/en/documents-publications/public-register/`, `register.consilium.europa.eu`): HTTP 403 "Browser check" for automated access. Document fetches from `data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/…` worked and were used.
- **Publications Office CELLAR SPARQL**: reachable, but returned no eighth-report work; the seventh report is also not retrievable by title string, so this is not independent evidence of non-publication. The committee page remains the authoritative check.
- **Council of Europe `rm.coe.int` PDF**: Cloudflare block on direct download; the Rosas speech was read through a text reader proxy and saved as `rosas_2025_seminar_speech.txt` (original URL recorded in the file).
- **AskTheEU request pages**: rendered as Atom feeds by the reader; full correspondence was retrieved from the HTML and the PDF attachments.
- **CURIA "Documents divers"** page: HTTP 403.

## Recommendation

The only route to 2023/2024/2025 annual counts is the eighth activity report when published, or a new FOI asking the Council specifically for the per-year table (not the opinions). The 12964/26 refusal shows the Council holds opinions, not an aggregate table, so a request should ask for "any table, schedule or statistics of opinions by calendar year and by first term/renewal" and, if none exists, for confirmation of that fact.
