# Inditex — board, nomination and CEO succession probe, 2019–2025

Entity: **Inditex** (Industria de Diseño Textil, S.A.), Spanish listed company, fiscal year ending 31 January.
Board: *Consejo de Administración*. The body that screens board candidates is the **Nomination Committee** (*Comisión de Nombramientos*); Inditex has **no combined nomination-and-remuneration committee** — the Remuneration Committee is a separate board committee, so the `corporate_nomination_committee` rows use the Nomination Committee alone.

## Year convention

* `period` = the calendar year in which the event occurred. AGM resolutions are attributed to the year the AGM was held (16 Jul 2019, 14 Jul 2020, 13 Jul 2021, 12 Jul 2022, 11 Jul 2023, 9 Jul 2024, 15 Jul 2025).
* Board co-options are attributed to the calendar year in which the appointment took effect (Anne Lange 10 Dec 2019; Óscar García Maceiras 29 Nov 2021; Marta Ortega Pérez 1 Apr 2022). Because a co-opted seat is later put to the AGM for ratification, each such seat is counted **once**, in the year of effect, following the definition; the ratification is not counted again.
* Departures are attributed to the calendar year of the effective leaving date, which can differ from the financial year in which the ACGR reports them (e.g. Pablo Isla's departure effective 31 Mar 2022 is reported in the ACGR for FY2022, tabled at the 2023 AGM).
* Nomination Committee composition and meeting counts are taken from the annual corporate governance report (ACGR) for financial year YYYY, i.e. the year running 1 February YYYY to 31 January YYYY+1; composition is stated as at the fiscal year-end, meetings as the count the report gives for the year.
* `corporate_board_openings` = seats filled by appointment/re-appointment at that year's AGM **plus** co-options effective in that year, renewals included (one seat per resolution, counted once). `corporate_board_new_members` = first-time members only. The two therefore differ only where a seat is filled by re-election of an incumbent.

## Notes on the six gaps

**Board openings.** 2019, 2021 and 2022 each combine an AGM round with a co-option. 2019 is the largest year (5 + 1 = 6): the 16 Jul 2019 AGM re-elected Pablo Isla Álvarez de Tejera, Amancio Ortega Gaona, Emilio Saracho Rodríguez de Torres and José Luis Durán Schulz and appointed Carlos Crespo González, and the board co-opted Anne Lange on 10 Dec 2019 to fill a vacancy, an appointment ratified at the 2020 AGM. 2021 counts 1 AGM re-election plus the 29 Nov 2021 co-option, and 2022 counts 2 AGM re-elections plus the co-option effective 1 Apr 2022. 2020 and 2023 have no co-option; 2024 and 2025 have only AGM resolutions.

**First-time members.** Intake is 2, 0, 1, 1, 0, 2, 1 across 2019–2025 — seven new faces in seven years against a board of 9–11 seats, i.e. roughly one new director a year. The 2024 pair needs care: Flora Pérez Marcote had attended the board since 9 Dec 2015 as the legal representative of Pontegadea Inversiones, S.L., and was elected as a director in her own name in 2024 after the legal-person directorship became ineligible; the company treats this as a new proprietary directorship, and it is counted as intake here on that basis.

**Departures.** 0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 1. The 2019 and 2020 zeros are explicit statements in the ACGRs, not derivations. The 2022 transition is the chair succession: Pablo Isla resigned on 29 Nov 2021 effective 31 Mar 2022, and Marta Ortega Pérez was co-opted as non-executive Chair effective 1 Apr 2022 and ratified at the AGM of 12 Jul 2022 — she is a new member in 2022, he a departure in 2022. Anne Lange's 2024 exit is an expiry of term recorded in the text rather than in the removals table (which lists only Pontegadea Inversiones, S.L.); both are counted. Emilio Saracho's 2023 exit was a resignation effective 11 Jul 2023 after he ceased to qualify as independent; the removals table gives 11/07/2023 as the date of termination and 16/07/2023 as the end of his term.

**Candidate list.** 1 in every year. The convening notice names every director put to the vote, and for 2019–2022 the notice itself lists, among the documents available to shareholders, a board explanatory report evaluating the candidates' skills, experience and merits. The notice does not print CVs; it names the candidates and points to that report (in 2023 also the updated board skills matrix) as published with the notice, which is what makes the slate visible before the vote. For 2023–2025 the notice names the candidates in the agenda and the ACGR records that the board's explanatory report on their suitability (and, in 2023, the updated board skills matrix) was published with the notice. No year in which candidates were announced only after election was found.

**Nomination Committee.** Five members in every year of the window, chaired by Emilio Saracho Rodríguez de Torres until 2022, José Luis Durán Schulz from 2022 to 2024, and Belén Romana García from 2024. Meeting counts: 5, 5, 4, 5, 6, 6, 6. Membership is stable and small; the committee's own reports on board needs are published with each AGM notice, which is the visible front end of the intake.

**CEO changes.** Two: Carlos Crespo González became CEO in 2019 when the chair and CEO roles were separated (Pablo Isla remained Executive Chairman), and on 29 Nov 2021 Crespo resigned as CEO and Óscar García Maceiras was appointed CEO, taking office with the board co-option the same day. The 2022 chair change is not a CEO change: Marta Ortega became non-executive Chair. The zero values for 2020 and 2022–2025 are supported by the appointment date recorded in each ACGR (29 Nov 2021, ratified 12 Jul 2022) and, for 2020, by the CEO's recorded appointment year (2019); no appointment or resignation is reported in those years.

## Access notes and failed attempts

* The corporate site did **not** block: all documents were fetched from `www.inditex.com` with HTTP 200, via the media endpoint pattern `https://www.inditex.com/itxcomweb/api/media/<uuid>/<filename>` that the investor pages use. The CNMV route was therefore not needed.
* `https://www.inditex.com/itxcomweb/en/investors/annual-reports` returns HTTP 200 but is a JavaScript shell: the HTML contains no document links, so the document list had to be read from the rendered page links of `.../investors/corporate-governance/reports-and-regulations` and `.../investors/corporate-governance/annual-general-meeting`.
* `https://annualreport.inditex.com/` redirects to `/anrpxxvui/en/home`, the annual-report viewer, which exposes only the current year (FY2025); its download centre links to `IAR_ENG.pdf`, `IAGC2025_ENG.pdf` and the consolidated accounts. `https://annualreport.inditex.com/en/` returned HTTP 500 and `https://static.inditex.com/annual_report_2024/en/index.html` returned HTTP 404.
* Year labels are inconsistent across Inditex documents: the 2019 and 2020 files are CNMV-format reports dated by year-end (`YEAR-END DATE: 31/01/2019`, `31/01/2020`), while later reports are labelled by fiscal year (`ACGR 2021` covers 1 Feb 2021 – 31 Jan 2022). Files here are named by fiscal year, so `inditex-acgr-fy2018.pdf` is the report with year-end 31/01/2019.
* Inditex's FY2025 ACGR (year-end 31/01/2026) was published in 2026 and is outside the 2019–2025 window; it is used only to corroborate the 2025 AGM and the 2025 committee figures.
