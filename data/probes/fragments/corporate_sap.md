# SAP SE — board succession and intake visibility, 2019–2025

SAP SE is a European Company (Societas Europaea) with a two-tier board: an Executive Board and an 18-member
Supervisory Board. Nine Supervisory Board seats are held by **shareholder representatives**, elected by the Annual
General Meeting; nine are held by **employee representatives**, appointed under the Agreement on the Involvement of
Employees in SAP SE (through the SE Works Council Europe and employee ballots), never voted at the AGM. Every count
below is for the **shareholder representatives only** unless a note says otherwise; the two sides are never summed.

Sources are SAP's own filed documents: Form 20-F annual reports filed with the SEC (FY2019–FY2025), the AGM voting
results published on sap.com, the AGM 2024 convening notice, the 2023 AGM candidate CV documents, and the Integrated
Report 2025. `sap.com` returns HTTP 403 to this client, so documents were retrieved from the Internet Archive's copy
of the same sap.com URL (`web.archive.org/web/<ts>id_/<original sap.com URL>`); the saved files hold the original
document bytes and the `source_url` column keeps the canonical company address. The 20-F filings were retrieved
directly from sec.gov.

## Year convention

Events are attributed to the **calendar year of the AGM** that decided them (AGM dates: 15 May 2019, 20 May 2020,
12 May 2021, 18 May 2022, 11 May 2023, 15 May 2024, 13 May 2025). Mid-year arrivals and departures outside an AGM
(court appointments, resignations, Works Council appointments) are attributed to the year in which the change took
effect. A seat is counted once: Qi Lu, appointed by the court on 21 Dec 2020 and ratified by shareholders at the 2021
AGM, is one appointment in 2020 and is not counted again in 2021.

`corporate_board_openings` = shareholder-representative seats put to a vote at that year's AGM, re-elections
included, one resolution per candidate. `corporate_board_new_members` = people joining the shareholder side who were
not on the board at the start of the year (reappointments excluded). `corporate_board_departures` = shareholders
leaving for any reason.

## Board size at each year-end

18 members every year (9 shareholder + 9 employee representatives). The FY2018 20-F Supervisory Board table shows
every one of the 18 mandates expiring in 2019, which is why all nine shareholder seats were voted at the 2019 AGM and
all nine employee seats were reappointed by the SE Works Council Europe on 3 Apr 2019
(`sources/official/probes/sap-20f-2018.tables.txt`, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1000184/000110465919011304/a18-38214_120f.htm). The split is fixed by the Articles of
Incorporation and the Agreement on the Involvement of Employees: "the Supervisory Board of SAP SE comprises 18
members, nine of whom will be elected by the General Meeting of Shareholders as shareholder representatives"
(AGM 2024 convening notice, p. 5/62).

## Nomination Committee

Full name: *Nominierungsausschuss* (Nomination Committee). It is composed exclusively of shareholder
representatives and proposes the candidates the Supervisory Board recommends to the AGM. Membership below is taken
from the footnote-7 markers of each 20-F Supervisory Board table (footnote 8 in the FY2025 20-F, which renumbers
the committees).

| year | members | chair | meetings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2019 | 4 — Plattner, Ala-Pietilä, Greene, Liautaud | not disclosed | not disclosed |
| 2020 | 4 — Plattner, Ala-Pietilä, Liautaud, Oswald | not disclosed | not disclosed |
| 2021 | 4 — Plattner, Evans, Rotsch, Westphal | not disclosed | not disclosed |
| 2022 | 5 — Plattner, Evans, Rotsch, Westphal, Wiedenfels | not disclosed | not disclosed |
| 2023 | 5 — Plattner, Evans, Rotsch, Westphal, Wiedenfels | not disclosed | not disclosed |
| 2024 | 5 — Ala-Pietilä, Evans, Rotsch, Westphal, Wiedenfels | Wiedenfels (chair until 6 Nov 2025) | not disclosed |
| 2025 | 4 — Ala-Pietilä, Evans, Rotsch, Westphal | Ala-Pietilä from 6 Nov 2025; Wiedenfels before | 7 |

The 2025 chair change and meeting count are stated in the Integrated Report 2025: "The Nomination Committee met
seven times in the reporting year", and the November 2025 Supervisory Board meeting approved "a change in the
membership of the Nomination Committee, which involved Pekka Ala-Pietilä taking over as Committee chairperson from
Gunnar Wiedenfels, who was leaving the Committee". The 2025 committee ran the search for the next Supervisory Board
chairperson with external consultants, working from a shortlist of assessed candidates — the clearest published
window on SAP's board intake process in this period.

## Candidate list visibility

SAP published a named, CV-bearing candidate list before the vote in every year for which an AGM election document
could be retrieved (2023, 2024). For 2019–2022 the value is left blank with status `partial`: the AGM voting records
show candidates were voted on individually by name (2019: items 6a–6i; 2022: four named individuals elected
18 May 2022), but the convening notices themselves could not be obtained, so publication of names *with CVs before
the vote* is not directly evidenced for those years. In years with no Supervisory Board election (2020, 2025) the
gap is `not_applicable`.

## Failed URL attempts and workarounds

- `https://www.sap.com/investors/en/reports.html` — HTTP 403 (WAF). Also 403: `/investors/en.html`, all
  `/docs/download/investors/...` PDFs, `/investors/en/general-meeting.html` (404 on that path).
- Wayback check of the sap.com PDF paths: the 2024 and 2025 AGM invitations and the 2025 Integrated Report were
  archived and were downloaded from `web.archive.org`; the 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 invitations
  (`…/investors/<year>/sap-se-agm-<year>-invitation.pdf`) and the 2019–2024 Integrated Reports
  (`…/investors/<year>/sap-<year>-integrated-report.pdf`) return 403 through the archive, i.e. they were never
  archived and the live WAF blocks the archive's fetch.
- `filings.xbrl.org` (ESEF) has no SAP SE filing: the API query returns an empty result set.
- `bundesanzeiger.de` search redirects in a loop for this client; SAP's convening notices could not be reached there.
- The SEC route worked: SAP SE (CIK 0001000184) files a 20-F each year, and the AGM 2025 voting results were filed as
  a 6-K exhibit (accession 0001104659-25-051206). Only the 2025 AGM results were filed this way; no 6-K with AGM
  results exists for 2019–2024.
- The archived sap.com "voting on resolutions" page covers the 2019 and 2020 AGMs only; later years are rendered
  client-side in the current page and no archived copy of the rendered tables exists.
