## Notes

### Year convention
- `corporate_board_openings`, `corporate_board_new_members`, `corporate_candidate_list`, `corporate_ceo_changes`: attributed to the calendar year in which the AGM was held (26 April 2019, 29 April 2020, 11 May 2021, 29 April 2022, 27 April 2023, 11 April 2024, 11 April 2025). The count is the number of separate director resolutions (5a, 5b, …) at that AGM.
- `corporate_board_departures`: attributed to the calendar year in which the director left, taken from the annual report of that fiscal year.
- `corporate_nomination_committee`: member count at 31 December of the fiscal year; meeting count for the full fiscal year. Names and dates of members joining or leaving mid-year are in the CSV notes.
- One document serves as both annual report and Form 20-F ("Annual Report & Form 20-F Information 20XX"); page numbers are the document's printed page numbers.

### Board size at each year-end (31 December)
| year | directors at 31 Dec | note |
|---|---|---|
| 2019 | 12 | 13 directors served, Rudy Markham retired 26 April 2019 |
| 2020 | 14 | 9 men, 5 women; Ashley and Layfield appointed during the year |
| 2021 | 13 | 8 men, 5 women; Berger, Chipchase and Dunoyer left, Sarin and Rummelt joined |
| 2022 | 13 | 8 men, 5 women; no change during the year |
| 2023 | 13 | 7 men, 6 women; Johansson retired, Manz joined |
| 2024 | 13 | no change during the year; the 2024 report's board page is dated 6 February 2025 and shows 15 directors after Haas and Conix joined |
| 2025 | 14 | 7 men, 7 women; DiSanzo and Rummelt retired, Haas, Conix and Knudsen joined |

Board size is reported for context only. No departure count in the CSV is derived from a board-size difference; each is taken from a stated retirement or step-down, or is recorded as 0 with status `partial` where the annual report discloses no departure.

### Nomination and Governance Committee
- Composition is not stated in a single sentence in the reports: members are given by the committee letters on the Board of Directors page and by the "Board Committee membership and meeting attendance" table. For 2024 and 2025 the evidence cell therefore quotes the attendance-table row for the Chair rather than a prose sentence; the full membership list is in the CSV notes.
- Meetings per fiscal year: 2019 five, 2020 five, 2021 five, 2022 seven, 2023 six, 2024 five, 2025 four. For 2019–2021 the count is stated in the committee report prose; for 2022–2025 it is the denominator in the attendance table.
- The Committee's exact name is "Nomination and Governance Committee" throughout 2019–2025.

### AGM document route
- Election counts come from the Notice of Meeting (one resolution per director, with a biography and CV for each candidate); the issued/re-elected split comes from the poll results published after each AGM, which label each resolution "To elect" or "To re-elect".
- The 2019 poll result was published as a stock-exchange-announcement page, not a PDF; it is saved as `.html` with a `.txt` rendition.
- `sources/official/probes/astrazeneca-agm-voting-update-2019.pdf` is the 2018-AGM remuneration-vote statement linked from the same shareholder page; it is not a 2019 AGM result and is not used as evidence.
