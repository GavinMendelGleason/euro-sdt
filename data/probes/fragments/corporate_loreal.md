## Notes

### Coverage

All seven years (2019-2025) and all six gaps are populated: 42 rows, every one with status `found`. Openings total 27 seats over the period, first-time members 11, departures 9, and there is one CEO change (2021).

### Year convention

Each row is attributed to the calendar year in which the ordinary general meeting was held (or, for the CEO, the year the change took effect). L'Oréal AGM dates: 18 April 2019; 30 June 2020 (postponed from 21 April 2020 because of the Covid-19 pandemic); 20 April 2021; 21 April 2022; 21 April 2023; 23 April 2024; 29 April 2025. Board size is reported at 31 December of each year, as published in the universal registration document of that year.

`corporate_board_openings` counts the seats filled by the appointment/re-appointment resolutions tabled at that year's AGM (renewals included); a seat is counted once, in the year the appointment took effect. L'Oréal co-opts no directors between AGMs in this period: every appointment in 2019-2025 was voted by the AGM or, for the two employee-representative seats, made by the CFE-CGC union and the European Works Council.

`corporate_board_departures` counts every seat vacated in the year (end of term, resignation, non-renewal), including the reserved employee-representative seats. `corporate_board_new_members` counts every first-time director, including the two employee representatives appointed in 2022; where the arrivals came through the employee channel rather than an AGM resolution this is stated in the CSV notes.

### Committee naming

The nominating committee is the `Comité des Nominations et de la Gouvernance`. The English translation used by the issuer changed: the 2019 universal registration document calls it the "Appointments and Governance Committee"; from the 2020 document onwards it is the "Nominations and Governance Committee". The name quoted in each CSV note is the one used in that year's document. The committee has four members in 2019-2024 and six in 2025 (three members joined at the close of the 29 April 2025 AGM). The chair passed from Sophie Bellon to Patrice Caine at the close of the 21 April 2023 AGM.

### Candidate list

L'Oréal publishes a candidate list before every vote: the convening notice carries a section 5, "Information concerning Directors whose appointment or renewal is proposed to the Annual General Meeting", giving for each candidate a biographical note, age, nationality, business address, shareholding, the offices held outside L'Oréal and the offices expired in the last five years. This is why `corporate_candidate_list` is 1 for all seven years. For 2023 the version of the convening notice published on the issuer's AGM page is the mid-section booklet only (agenda and draft resolutions, candidates named without biographies); the biographies of the two candidates standing for renewal are in the 2022 universal registration document filed on 16 March 2023, before the vote.

### Text extraction note

Each PDF is saved three times in `sources/official/probes/`: `<name>.pdf`, `<name>.txt` produced with `pdftotext -layout`, and `<name>.raw.txt` produced with plain `pdftotext`. The layout text interleaves the two columns of the corporate-governance pages, so a sentence that runs across the column break is not contiguous there; every quotation in the table above was checked as a contiguous string against one of the two text files of the document named in `source_file`, and can be read on the cited PDF page. Where a value rests on more than one verbatim sentence, the cell quotes each of them, separated by `|`; in the committee rows the fragments are rows of the composition table.
