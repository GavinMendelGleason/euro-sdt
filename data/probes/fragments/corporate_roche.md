## Year convention

Events are attributed to the calendar year of the Annual General Meeting (AGM), not to the financial year whose accounts the meeting approves. Each annual report covers one financial year ending 31 December and reviews the AGM held in March of that same year — the 2023 annual report contains “Review AGM 2023” for the meeting of 14 March 2023 — so the annual report of year N supplies the AGM facts for period N. Roche elected its whole board for one-year terms in every year of the period, so `corporate_board_openings` equals the number of director-election resolutions put to that AGM, renewals included. No co-opted director appears in any “Composition as at 31 December” table, and where a member left mid-year the seat stayed vacant until the next AGM (P. R. Voser from July 2019, J. Brown from October 2022), so no seat is filled twice and the co-option convention plays no role here.

## Chair and CEO

The chair transition from Dr Christoph Franz to Dr Severin Schwan took place at the AGM of **14 March 2023**, not 2022: “As successor of Dr Christoph Franz, shareholders elected Dr Severin Schwan as a member of the Board of Directors (member since 2013) and as the new Chairman of the Board of Directors for a term of one year as provided by the Articles of Incorporation.” (annual report 2023, p. 126). Dr Franz announced in July 2022 that he would not stand for re-election at the AGM in March 2023 (annual report 2022, p. 162), which is why the announcement and the change fall in different years. The CEO change falls at the same meeting: “Effective 14 March 2023, the Board of Directors appointed Dr Thomas Schinecker, former CEO Roche Diagnostics, as Dr Schwan’s successor as the CEO Roche Group.” (annual report 2023, p. 130). Dr Schwan therefore moved from CEO to Chairman on one day, and the 2023 AGM counted as one board opening for him, not two.

## Roche family pooling agreement

The corporate governance chapter marks the two family representatives on the board: “André Hoffmann (1958) (representative of the shareholder group with pooled voting rights)” and “Dr Jörg Duschmalé (1984) (representative of the shareholder group with pooled voting rights)” (annual report 2024, p. 166). The group’s weight is stated in the remuneration report: the persons “are closely associated with them belong to a contractually bound shareholder group with pooled voting rights. At the end of 2024 this group held 69,318,000 shares (64.97% of issued shares).” (annual report 2024, p. 200). In the passages examined the Articles of Incorporation reserve no seat for the group; both representatives stand for annual election with the rest of the board, and both are counted in the opening and re-election figures above.

## Rows marked partial

`corporate_board_departures` for 2021 and 2025, and `corporate_ceo_changes` for 2019 and 2020, carry status `partial`.
