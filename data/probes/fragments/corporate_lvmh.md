## Notes

**Year convention.** A row is attributed to the calendar year in which the event took effect, which for director elections is the year of the AGM that voted the resolution (LVMH holds its combined AGM in April, or 30 June 2020 because of Covid-19). A row therefore describes calendar year N (the fiscal year of the URD that reports it), and the residency of directorships is measured at 31 December N. Documents used: URD for fiscal year N (published the following March, reporting changes during N and the resolutions in force) plus, where available, the convening brochure for the AGM held during N.

**Co-option convention.** Wei Sun Christianson was co-opted by the Board on 18 April 2024 and her co-option was ratified by the AGM of 17 April 2025 (5th resolution). Her seat is counted once, in 2024 (the year the appointment took effect), in `corporate_board_openings` and in `corporate_board_new_members`; the 2025 ratification is not counted again.

**Employee-representative directors.** LVMH's board includes directors representing employees, appointed by the Group Works Council / SE Works Council rather than elected by the AGM (Dominique Aumont and Marie-Véronique Belloeil-Melkin in 2020, Lydia Zune to take effect in 2026). They are counted in `corporate_board_new_members` (first-time members) but not in `corporate_board_openings`, which is defined as AGM election resolutions.

**Gaps and conventions in individual rows.**

* `corporate_board_openings` counts every director-election resolution at the AGM (renewals included) plus any seat filled by co-option during the year, per the convention above. 2019: 4 renewals + 1 appointment = 5. 2020: 5 renewals + 1 appointment = 6. 2021: 4 renewals = 4. 2022: 4 renewals = 4. 2023: 5 renewals + 1 appointment = 6. 2024: 1 renewal + 3 appointments + 1 co-option = 5. 2025: 4 renewals = 4.
