## Notes

### Year convention

Every row is keyed to the calendar year of the Annual General Meeting: 2019 AGM 11 April 2019, 2020 AGM 23 April 2020, 2021 AGM 15 April 2021, 2022 AGM 7 April 2022, 2023 AGM 20 April 2023, 2024 AGM 18 April 2024, 2025 AGM 16 April 2025. Elections, first-time members and end-of-term departures decided at an AGM are attributed to that AGM's year. Events between AGMs are attributed to the year in which they took effect: Schneider's exit from the Board and Freixe's appointment as CEO both on 1 September 2024; Freixe's exit from the Executive Board and Navratil's appointment as CEO on 1 September 2025; Bulcke's exit from the Board on 1 October 2025. Committee member counts and meeting counts are the fiscal-year disclosures (1 January to 31 December) of the Corporate Governance Report for that year.

### Board size at each year-end

Counted from the published roster "Board of Directors of Nestlé S.A. at December 31, YYYY" (Annual Review, governance pages; reproduced in the Corporate Governance Report).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| directors | 14 | 14 | 14 | 14 | 15 | 13 | 11 |

The Board sat at 14 for four years, peaked at 15 after the 2023 AGM (Blair and Ineichen-Fleisch in, Cheng out), fell to 13 by 31 December 2024 (de Castries and Ross retired at the AGM, Matchett elected, then Schneider left the Board on 1 September 2024 when he ceased to be CEO), and to 11 by 31 December 2025 (Freixe elected at the AGM; Jimenez de Mora died 26 June 2025; Bulcke stepped down 1 October 2025; Freixe's own Board mandate ended at some point in 2025 that no saved document dates). These are rosters, not departure counts: the `corporate_board_departures` rows count only named exits.

### Chair change: the 2019 attribution in the brief is not supported

The brief asks for "the 2019 chair change from Peter Brabeck-Letmathe to Paul Bulcke". The sources place that handover in 2017, not 2019. The AGM 2019 invitation already proposes "Mr Paul Bulcke as member and as Chairman of the Board of Directors", and the AGM 2026 invitation records "He served as Nestlé S.A.'s CEO from 2008 to 2016 and as Chair of the Board of Directors of Nestlé S.A. from 2017 to 2025." No 2019 chair event appears in any saved document, so none is recorded. The only chair change inside the window is Bulcke to Pablo Isla: "Pablo Isla succeeded Paul Bulcke as Chair of Nestlé S.A. effective October 1, 2025." The chair is not a separate gap in the contract; the change appears as Bulcke's 2025 departure.

### Laurent Freixe and the 2025 departure count

Freixe was elected a director at the AGM of 16 April 2025, ceased to be CEO on 1 September 2025, is absent from the 31 December 2025 Board roster, takes no director fee in the 2025 compensation table, and is excluded from the 2026 re-election slate ("The Board proposes the re-election of all its members, with the exception of Laurent Freixe, our former CEO"). No saved document states the date his Board mandate ended, and Swiss law would in any case have required his mandate to run to the 2026 AGM unless he resigned. `corporate_board_departures` for 2025 is therefore `partial` at 2 — Bulcke and Jimenez de Mora, both explicitly dated — with the third exit noted but not counted.

### CEO sequence

Ulf Mark Schneider was CEO throughout 2019-2023 and until 31 August 2024, so `corporate_ceo_changes` is 0 for 2019-2023. The brief's note that Freixe took over "in Sept 2024" is right and the effective date governs: "Laurent Freixe was appointed CEO effective September 1, 2024." That change is keyed to 2024, not 2025. The 2025 change is Navratil: "He was appointed CEO effective September 1, 2025." Every year in the window therefore has at most one CEO change, and the two changes fall in 2024 and 2025.

### Nomination committee naming across the window

- 2019-2020: "Nomination and Sustainability Committee", chaired by Henri de Castries; 4 members at end-2019 (Bulcke, de Castries, Veneman, Cheng), 5 at end-2020 (Paliwal added).
- 2021-2024: "Nomination Committee", sustainability spun into a separate Sustainability Committee in 2021; 4 members each year, chaired by Henri de Castries until the 2024 AGM, then by Pablo Isla (2024: Bulcke, Isla, Paliwal, Ineichen-Fleisch).
- 2025: "Nomination Committee", 3 members (Isla chair, Ineichen-Fleisch, Paliwal) after Bulcke left the Board.
- From the 2026 AGM the Board merges nomination and governance into a "Nomination and Corporate Governance Committee" chaired by Dick Boer. That falls outside the 2019-2025 window and is recorded only because the 2025 report announces it in forward-looking text.
