## Notes

- `corporate_board_openings` counts every Supervisory Board appointment/reappointment resolution at that year's AGM, so renewed terms are included and new intake is separated out in `corporate_board_new_members`.
- 2019 and 2024 departures are recorded as 0 with status `partial`: no departure appears in the governance report and every expiring term was renewed, but no document states a zero explicitly.
- 2020 board grew from eight to nine members when Durcan and East were appointed alongside Aris's reappointment; 2021 shrank back to eight when Grose and Smits-Nusteling retired and only Conix was nominated.
- The 2024 AGM was also a Board of Management transition: co-presidents Peter Wennink and Martin van den Brink retired and Christophe Fouquet became sole President and CEO; Jim Koonmen joined as Chief Customer Officer in a newly created role. Counted once under `corporate_ceo_changes`.
- Selection and Nomination Committee meetings per year: 2019 = 4, 2020 = 6, 2021 = 9, 2022 = 6, 2023 = 6, 2024 = 5, 2025 = 4.
- Candidate publication: the AGM agenda and explanatory notes give each nominee's CV, other positions, independence statement and the Works Council position, so `corporate_candidate_list` = 1 for every year.
