# Siemens AG — Supervisory Board and Managing Board succession, fiscal 2019–2025

**Entity:** `Siemens` (frame row: *Siemens, Industrials, Germany*). **Bodies covered:** the *Supervisory Board* (Aufsichtsrat, 20 seats: 10 shareholder representatives elected by the Annual Shareholders' Meeting, 10 employee representatives elected under the German Codetermination Act), the *Managing Board* (Vorstand; its chair is the President and CEO), and the Supervisory Board's ***Nominating Committee*** (*Nominierungsausschuss*), which recommends the shareholder-representative candidates for election.

Fragment: `data/probes/fragments/corporate_siemens.csv`. Every value is a supervisory-board / managing-board count; **shareholder representatives and employee representatives are never summed** (see Conventions).

## Conventions

1. **Year convention.** `period` is the **Siemens fiscal year** (October 1 – September 30). The ordinary AGM that belongs to fiscal year *Y* is held in January/February of the *same calendar year* *Y*, so `period` also equals the calendar year of the AGM: AGM of Jan 30, 2019 → fiscal 2019; Feb 5, 2020 → 2020; Feb 3, 2021 → 2021; Feb 10, 2022 → 2022; Feb 9, 2023 → 2023; Feb 8, 2024 → 2024; Feb 13, 2025 → 2025. Departures and appointments are attributed to the fiscal year in which they took effect (e.g. the Feb 9, 2023 departures fall in fiscal 2023).
2. **Shareholder representatives only.** `corporate_board_openings`, `corporate_board_new_members` and `corporate_board_departures` count **shareholder representatives**; the AGM elects only those ten seats. Employee-representative movements are listed separately below and are **not** added to any value.
3. **Openings** = seats filled by election/re-election resolutions at that year's AGM, renewals included (e.g. 2023 = 7 resolutions: 3 new + 4 re-elected; 2025 = 5 resolutions: 1 new + 4 re-elected, one of them an early re-election).
4. **Zero-opening years** are a documented absence, not an estimate: the convening notice's agenda and the published voting results for 2019, 2020, 2022 and 2024 contain **no** resolution on the election of Supervisory Board members (grep for “election of members to the Supervisory Board” returns 0 hits in both documents of each of those years). The verbatim cell quotes the last agenda item of that AGM; the agenda and the full resolution list are in the cited documents.
5. **Quotations** are verbatim from the saved PDFs; line breaks and PDF hyphenation have been removed, nothing else changed.

## Nomination body

The **Nominating Committee** (*Nominierungsausschuss*) of the Supervisory Board recommends the shareholder-representative candidates to the plenary board; the plenary then proposes them to the AGM. Disclosure pattern: the annual report / corporate governance statement names the members “as of September 30” and the Report of the Supervisory Board states how often the committee met in the fiscal year. Composition and meeting counts are carried in the CSV `notes` column for each year: 4 members and chair Jim Hagemann Snabe through fiscal 2024, then **5 members with Dr. Werner Brandt as chairman as of September 30, 2025**; meetings 1 (2019), 6 (2020), 1 (2021), 7 (2022), 3 (2023), 3 (2024), 7 (2025). The committee is supported by an external consulting firm in candidate search (disclosed for fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025).

## Chair and CEO: what the documents actually show

- **Supervisory Board chairman: Jim Hagemann Snabe, unchanged 2019–2025.** He was re-elected to the board and as chairman in 2021 (“the Supervisory Board reelected Mr. Snabe its Chairman”) and again in 2025, the latter for a two-year term: “Jim Hagemann Snabe, whose term of office also expired, as scheduled, at the conclusion of the Annual Shareholders' Meeting on February 13, 2025, was reelected a shareholder representative on the Supervisory Board to a two-year term of office, which will run until the conclusion of the 2027 Annual Shareholders' Meeting. The Supervisory Board also reelected him to serve as its Chairman.” (FY 2025 report). So the 2021 event was a **CEO** change only — there was **no** supervisory-chair handover in 2021, and none to Roland Busch at any point.
- **President and CEO: Joe Kaeser → Dr. Roland Busch**, effective the end of the AGM on February 3, 2021: “Dr. Roland Busch was appointed President and CEO effective the end of the 2021 Annual Shareholders' Meeting.” (AR 2021); “Joe Kaeser left the Managing Board effective the end of the Annual Shareholders' Meeting on February 3, 2021.” (AR 2021). Busch is named President and CEO for the whole of fiscal 2025.
- **Hermann Requardt did not take any Siemens board chair.** He appears in the saved documents only as a *former Managing Board member* (until Jan 31, 2015) in the pension/compensation tables; he is not in any Supervisory Board membership table 2019–2025. The brief's suggested “handover to Jim Snabe/Hermann Requardt” has no documentary basis — Snabe held the chair continuously.
