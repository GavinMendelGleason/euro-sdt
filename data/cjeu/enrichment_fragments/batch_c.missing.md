## Source composition

`sources/official/cjeu-priit-pikamae.txt` and `sources/official/cjeu-koen-lenaerts.txt` append the opening of the next member's biography after the target member's own section. Every quote in `batch_c.csv` was taken from the target member's own section.

Koen Lenaerts' memberships and honorary titles are recorded in the current Court of Justice page (`sources/official/cjeu-court-members-current.txt`), which is fuller than the condensed `sources/cjeu/koen-lenaerts.txt`; the condensed file is used only for qualifications and the fellowship that the current page does not list.
