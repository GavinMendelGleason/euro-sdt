# German Marshall Fund

## Profile

- atlanticist
- us_linked
- Primarily funded by the German government (Federal Foreign Office) as a gift to the US in recognition of the Marshall Plan; also US foundations (including Robert Bosch, MacArthur), US corporations, and US government grants (State Dept, USAID, NED)
- Founded 1972 to strengthen transatlantic ties through research, exchanges and grants. Despite the German name, headquartered in Washington DC and closely aligned with US State Department foreign policy priorities. Key programmes include the Brussels Forum (annual) and Alliance for Securing Democracy. Commissioners with ties: Federica Mogherini, Cecilia Malmström.
