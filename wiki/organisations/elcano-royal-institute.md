# Elcano Royal Institute

## Profile

- Spanish government (Ministry of Foreign Affairs majority funder), Spanish corporate sector, no US government funding
- Spain's leading foreign and security policy think tank. Governed by a cross-party board including all living Spanish Prime Ministers, current government ministers, and major Spanish corporations. Funded primarily by Spanish state ministries (Foreign Affairs, Defence, Economy) and Spanish corporate sector — no US government, NATO or foreign government funding. Publishes on transatlantic relations and NATO as part of a broad foreign policy mandate, but has no structural Atlanticist alignment. Post-mandate connections to Miguel Arias Cañete (Juncker) and Josep Borrell (VdL I) reflect their status as leading Spanish foreign policy figures, not transatlantic network membership. Reclassified as non-Atlanticist April 2026 after primary source review.
