# New Direction – Foundation for European Conservatism

## Profile

- Partly funded by European Parliament (as ECR family foundation). Registered Belgium.
- Official think tank of the European Conservatives and Reformists (ECR) group. Thatcherite/Reagan conservative orientation; pro-NATO, pro-transatlantic but also Eurosceptic. Raffaele Fitto (Commission II, Executive VP) was on Board 2016–2024. Not a US NGO or lobby group.
