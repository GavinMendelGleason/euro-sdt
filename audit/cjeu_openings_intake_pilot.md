## What can be computed

### Openings per position stock

There are 92 current CJEU positions (27 Court judges + 11 AGs + 53 General Court judges + 1 Registrar). The 2024 intake of 11 is 12.0% of current stock.

### Institution share of 2024 intake

- Cambridge: 2/11 = 18.2%
- ENA: 2/11 = 18.2%
- College of Europe: 1/11 = 9.1%

These are shares of one intake cohort, not institution placement rates.

## What cannot be computed

The overproduction ratio requires:

\[
\frac{\text{aspirants}_t}{\text{openings}_t}
\]

The aspirant pool is missing. Therefore:

- Cannot estimate competition per CJEU seat.
- Cannot estimate placement rates for College of Europe, ENA, Cambridge, etc.
- Cannot distinguish a larger credentialed pool from a stable one.
- Cannot compare 2024 intake composition with the underlying applicant pool.

## Conclusion

A descriptive openings/intake analysis is possible. The SDT overproduction analysis is not yet possible without a candidate/aspirant denominator.
