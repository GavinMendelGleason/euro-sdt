## Limitations

- This is a seven-company pilot, not the frame census.
- LVMH does not state an individual role for every board member; those rows are `Board member`.
- Siemens now covers both the Managing Board and the Supervisory Board.
- Start and end dates are unknown; roles are dated by the page snapshot only.
- The frame remains the legacy 53-company list; 7/53 companies are covered.
- `check_v2.py` passes after import; the wiki and graph exports have not been regenerated for this pilot.
