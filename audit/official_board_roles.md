- Start and end dates are mostly unknown; roles are dated by the page/PDF snapshot only.

- Saint-Gobain was captured from its official 2025 Universal Registration Document because the live governance page is behind Cloudflare.
- Some official pages were reached through reader/proxy access; their source URLs remain the official company URLs.
- `check_v2.py` passes; the wiki and graph exports have not been regenerated for this capture.
