## Limitations

- This is a four-company pilot, not the frame census.
- LVMH does not state an individual role for every board member; those rows are `Board member`.
- Siemens covers the Managing Board, not the Supervisory Board.
- Start and end dates are unknown; the role is dated by the page snapshot only.
- The frame remains the legacy 53-company list; 4/53 companies are covered.
- `check_v2.py` passes after import; the wiki and graph exports have not been regenerated for this pilot.
