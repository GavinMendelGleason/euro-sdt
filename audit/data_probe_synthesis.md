# Data probe synthesis

## Formal candidate pools

| Track | Formal pool | Openings | Ratio | Evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| CJEU 2019–2022 | 50 first-term candidates | 45 | 1.11 | Article 255 reports |
| Commission 2024 | 27 formal nominees | 27 seats | 1.00 | Council list / Commission designates |

Neither ratio is an overproduction measure: both count formal nominees, not the wider eligible pool.

## National CJEU pools

Most member states publish only the final nominee. Applicant/shortlist data found:

| State | Post | Pool |
|---|---|---:|
| Sweden | General Court 2024 | 9 applicants, 3 shortlisted |
| Lithuania | General Court 2024 | 4 applicants, 3 shortlisted |
| Poland | Court of Justice 2023 | 6 admissible candidates |
| Poland | General Court 2023 | 7 admissible candidates |
| Portugal | Court of Justice 2024 | 3-name shortlist |
| Croatia | General Court 2024 | 2 proposed candidates for 2 seats |

These are not comparable national candidate pools. No state published an applicant count for a 2023–2025 Court of Justice seat.

## CJEU post-2022

- Eighth Article 255 report: **in progress**.
- Fourth panel aggregate: 70 opinions; 33 first-term / 37 renewal derived, not published.
- Annual 2023–2025 counts: **not public**.
- Council FOI released only seven redacted sample opinions.

## Commission

- 27 Commissioner seats / 27 final formal nominees.
- DG/DDG applicant counts: **not public**.
- EPSO reports competition laureates, not management-position applicant pools.

## Corporate

- 10 companies; 420 company-year rows.
- Board seats, departures, new members, CEO changes extracted.
- AGM nominee lists are published; **applicant/candidate pools are not**.
- The “openings” column includes seats up for election/reappointment, not only vacancies, and must not be treated as annual vacancy counts.

## Judicial eligibility

- 108 country-measure rows for 27 member states.
- 50 found, 11 partial, 17 not public, 3 blocked, 27 not applicable.
- Supreme-court, constitutional-court, law-professor and senior-advocate series are not additive.

## College of Europe

- No annual graduate series.
- Enrolment and completion rates only.
- 2024–25 bounded production: roughly 446–496.

## Conclusion

Public data support **formal candidate parity** in the CJEU and Commission. They do not support a general elite-overproduction claim, because the wider eligible pools and applicant pools are not public.
