## Caveats

- Counts are person-institution presences, not degree rows; a person with two degrees from one institution counts once.
- Multiple institutions can appear in one biography sentence; strings were split on semicolons and normalized.
- Institution names were canonicalized conservatively; residual variants remain in the CSV.
- This is descriptive association, not a causal effect of institution on job type.
- Registrar is included in the aggregate but excluded from the three job-type tables.
