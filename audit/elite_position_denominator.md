# Elite-position denominator

## Formula

\[
D = A + B + C
\]

- **A** = European Commission establishment-plan posts, all grades
- **B** = lawyers admitted to the bar in Germany, France, and Italy
- **C** = board members/seats of publicly traded companies in Germany, France, and Italy

## Central values

| Component | Value | Reference | Unit |
|---|---:|---|---|
| A: Commission posts | 23,357 | 2026 budget | posts |
| B: bar-admitted lawyers | 471,869 | DE 2026; FR 2024; IT 2025 | persons |
| C: listed-company board seats | 8,110 | 2025–26 | seats |
| **D** | **503,336** | mixed | — |

\[
23{,}357 + 471{,}869 + 8{,}110 = 503{,}336
\]

## A: Commission

- **23,357 authorised establishment-plan posts** in the 2026 budget, all grades AD/AST/AST-SC, permanent and temporary.
- Includes 41 posts added by Draft Amending Budget 2/2026; the unamended 2026 figure is 23,316.
- **22,730 posts were occupied** on 1 April 2026; the 23,357 figure is authorised posts, not filled posts.
- This is not the 33,071 staff headcount, which includes contract agents, local staff, seconded experts and other categories.

## B: lawyers

| Country | Value | Date | Definition |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Germany | 167,547 | 1 Jan 2026 | natural persons admitted to the Rechtsanwaltschaft |
| France | 75,681 | 1 Jan 2024 | avocats inscrits au tableau |
| Italy | 228,641 | 31 Dec 2025 | lawyers on the albi and Cassa Forense |
| **Total** | **471,869** | — | — |

France uses the justice-ministry series because it is the only French figure verified to the unit. The CNB figure is 77,598 for 2025; substituting it gives 473,786. Italy includes 17,177 pensioner contributors; restricting to practising non-pensioners gives 211,464 and a B of 454,692.

## C: listed boards

| Country | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Germany | 3,090 | 2,040 observed seats in 183 large caps + 233 smaller companies at 4.5 seats |
| France | 3,100 | SBF 120 at 13 directors + 220 other Euronext Paris issuers at 7 seats |
| Italy | 1,920 | 198 Euronext Milan issuers × 9.73 average seats |
| **Total** | **8,110** | main regulated markets only |

These are **board seats**, not unique people: one person with two mandates counts twice. Germany includes 803 employee representatives; the shareholder-side count is 1,235. The German and French totals are estimates because only the large-cap subset is directly observed. Adding SME growth venues would raise C to roughly 11,100.

## Sensitivity

| Scenario | A | B | C | D |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Low: filled posts, practising Italian lawyers, observed board subset | 22,730 | 454,692 | 5,390 | **482,812** |
| Central | 23,357 | 471,869 | 8,110 | **503,336** |
| High: authorised posts, CNB France, main + growth boards | 23,357 | 473,786 | 11,100 | **508,243** |

## Interpretation limits

- This is a **single mixed-year stock**, not an annual time series. A and B/C are not from the same reference date.
- The units differ: Commission **posts**, lawyer **persons**, board **seats**. A person can hold multiple board seats; a Commission post is not a person; a lawyer can also be a board member.
- No de-duplication across components is possible with these aggregates.
- The denominator is a scale index of elite positions, not a count of unique individuals.
