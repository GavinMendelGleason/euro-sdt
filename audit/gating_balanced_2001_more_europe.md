# Balanced six-institution panel with more European institutions

The fixed 1990–2024 panel has only two European institutions (College of Europe and Stockholm) plus Harvard and Georgetown. To add European coverage while retaining a fixed panel, I added Bocconi and LUISS from 2001.

## Panel

- College of Europe — matriculation
- Harvard — LL.M. degrees
- Stockholm — degrees/examinations
- Georgetown — degrees
- Bocconi — institution-wide degrees
- LUISS — institution-wide degrees

Window: **2001–2024**, fixed `n=6`. Four of the six are European.

![Six-institution Europe panel](assets/papers/methodology/gating-balanced-2001-more-europe.png)
