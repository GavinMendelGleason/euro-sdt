# CJEU judicial positions by year

Series used for the cutoff position denominators.

| Year | Positions | Composition |
|---:|---:|---|
| 2000–2003 | 39 | 15 ECJ judges + 9 AGs + 15 General Court |
| 2004–2006 | 58 | 25 + 8 + 25 |
| 2007–2012 | 62 | 27 + 8 + 27 |
| 2013–2014 | 65 | 28 + 9 + 28 |
| 2015 | 79 | 28 + 11 + 40 |
| 2016–2018 | 86 | 28 + 11 + 47 |
| 2019 | 93 | 28 + 11 + 54 |
| 2020–2025 | 92 | 27 + 11 + 54 |

Source: CURIA, *History of the Court* (https://curia.europa.eu/site/jcms/d2_5100/en/history), accession and reform dates. Values are year-end stocks. The 2013 ninth Advocate General arrived in October; the 2015 General Court reform and additional AGs are treated as effective for the 2015 year-end.

File: `data/denominator/judicial_positions_annual.csv`.
