# Coverage by College of Europe and the N≥5 panel

## Definition

`N≥5` means institutions with at least five educated elite people in the pooled non-MEP education frame. There are **23** such institutions. The panel includes the College of Europe.

Coverage is the share of educated people in a track with at least one education link to:

- the College of Europe;
- any N≥5 institution;
- the College only, with no other N≥5 institution.

## Results

| Track | Educated N | College | College-only | Panel N≥5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Commission + DG/DDG | 197 | 11 (5.58%) | 8 (4.06%) | 59 (29.95%) |
| Jurists | 98 | 7 (7.14%) | 6 (6.12%) | 58 (59.18%) |

Panel excluding the College:

- Commission + DG/DDG: 51/197 = **25.89%**
- Jurists: 52/98 = **53.06%**
