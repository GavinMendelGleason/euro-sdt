# Coverage by College of Europe and the N≥5 panel

Coverage is a binary indicator: a person either has any recorded education link to the College of Europe, or does not.

`N≥5` means institutions with at least five educated elite people in the pooled non-MEP education frame. There are 23 such institutions. The panel includes the College.

| Track | Educated N | College of Europe | Panel N≥5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Commission + DG/DDG | 197 | 11 (5.58%) | 59 (29.95%) |
| Jurists | 98 | 7 (7.14%) | 58 (59.18%) |
