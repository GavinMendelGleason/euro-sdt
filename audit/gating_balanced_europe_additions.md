# UCLouvain and Sciences Po additions

Both institutions have official degree series, but neither can enter the 2001–2024 six-institution panel without shortening it.

## UCLouvain — degrees awarded, 2001–2020

CRef annual yearbooks, *Diplômes décernés*, all domains, institution-wide, academic-year start.

Fixed seven-institution panel: College of Europe, Harvard, Stockholm, Georgetown, Bocconi, LUISS, UCLouvain.

![UCLouvain panel](assets/papers/methodology/gating_balanced_2001_2020_uclouvain.png)

- 2001: **747.6** per 1,000 Commission + judicial positions
- 2020: **1,305.4**
- `n=7`, fixed
- No CRef source for 2021–2024; series stops at 2020-21.

## Sciences Po — degrees awarded, 2015–2022

SIES/SISE, all diplomas delivered, institution-wide.

Fixed eight-institution panel: College, Harvard, Stockholm, Georgetown, Bocconi, LUISS, UCLouvain, Sciences Po.

![Sciences Po panel](assets/papers/methodology/gating_balanced_2015_2020_sciencespo.png)

- 2015: **1,290.3** per 1,000 positions
- 2019: **1,482.2**
- 2020: **1,435.2**
- `n=8`, fixed
- Sciences Po data cover 2015–2022 only; the panel is limited to the overlap with UCLouvain, 2015–2020.

## Consequence

- UCLouvain extends the European panel but reduces it to **2001–2020**.
- Sciences Po forces a **2015–2020** window because its data begin in 2015 and UCLouvain ends in 2020.
- Neither can extend the 2001–2024 panel.
- ENA remains unavailable for a fixed annual panel because its output begins in 2019 and 2023 is missing.
