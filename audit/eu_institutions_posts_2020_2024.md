# All-institution establishment-plan posts, 2020–2024

Source: General Statement of Expenditure, overview table of establishment plans of Union institutions and bodies, annual General Budget of the EU.

Scope: institutions and bodies in the EU General Budget — institutions, decentralised agencies, European joint undertakings, EIT and executive agencies. Excludes EIB and ECB, which are outside the General Budget.
