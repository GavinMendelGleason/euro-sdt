# CJEU formal overproduction pilot

## Formal candidate pool

The Article 255 panel assesses candidates formally proposed by member states. It distinguishes first-term candidates from renewals.

| Period | First-term candidates | Renewals | Openings | First-term candidates per opening |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2019 | 21 | 6 | 19 | **1.11** |
| 2020–2022 | 29 | 24 | 26 | **1.12** |
| **Total** | **50** | **30** | **45** | **1.11** |

Opening counts include Court of Justice judges, Advocates General and General Court judges. Renewals are excluded from openings.

## Interpretation

Formal competition was close to parity: about **1.11 first-term candidates per opening**.

This is not a measure of elite overproduction. The Article 255 pool contains only candidates formally proposed by member states, not the wider pool of eligible national judges, law professors, and senior lawyers. Overproduction can exist upstream of nomination.

## Data caveats

- 2022 candidate data cover January–February only; 2022 openings cover the full year.
- 2023–2025 candidate-pool data are not yet available in a published activity report.
- Candidate opinions are not matched one-to-one to individual openings in this pilot.
- Renewal candidates are counted separately and excluded from the ratio.
