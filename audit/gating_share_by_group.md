# Gating-institution education by elite group

## Definition

Share of all people in each group with at least one verified education link to one of the gating institutions.

Core set (10): College of Europe, Harvard University, University of Cambridge, ENA/INSP, LSE, UCLouvain, University of Oxford, Vilnius University, École Polytechnique, University of Cologne.

Bocconi is shown separately because it enters through the economic-elite investigation.

## Results

| Group | Core set | Core % | Including Bocconi | Including % |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Commission heads (commissioners, all terms) | 20/150 | 13.3% | 22/150 | 14.7% |
| Commission + DGs/DDGs | 39/235 | 16.6% | 41/235 | 17.4% |
| Judiciary (CJEU judges, AGs, General Court, registrar) | 32/98 | 32.7% | 33/98 | 33.7% |

## Notes

- Denominators are all people in the group, not only people with education coverage. A missing education record counts as no gating link.
- Counts are unique people with at least one gating-institution education record; multiple degrees at gating institutions count once.
- "Commission heads" means commissioners across the seven commission terms in the frame, not the current 27-member College.
- The judiciary result is much higher than the executive results.
- This is a descriptive association, not an appointment probability or a causal gate effect.
