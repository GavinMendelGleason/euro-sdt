# Gating-institution cutoffs and output coverage

## Definition

Cutoffs use education mentions summed over **Commissioners + DGs/DDGs + Judiciary**. Counts are people with an accepted education link, not degree rows.

File: `data/analysis/gating_cutoff_membership.csv`.

## Membership and current output coverage

| Cutoff | Institutions | Current output coverage | Mentions covered |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ≥5 | 18 | 6 | 59/132 = 44.7% |
| ≥4 | 29 | 6 | 59/176 = 33.5% |
| ≥3 | 42 | 6 | 59/215 = 27.4% |

The six with current output series are College of Europe, Harvard, ENA/INSP, Oxford, LSE and Cologne. Their output measures are mixed: matriculation, degrees, enrolment, examined candidates and first-examination passes.

The College-only line is not a good proxy: it overstates the composite by roughly two-thirds. It is also matriculation, not degrees.

## What is still required

A real overproduction test needs total output series for the missing institutions, preferably degrees awarded, plus the position denominator. The current panel does not yet contain those series, so no cutoff-specific overproduction estimate is reported here.

The most tractable first expansion is **cutoff ≥5**: 12 additional institutions. Even that requires heterogeneous national sources and a decision on whether “total output” means all degrees or only the elite programme.
