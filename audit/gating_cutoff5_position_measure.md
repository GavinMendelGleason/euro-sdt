# Cutoff ≥5 output per Commission + judiciary position

## Construction

- Numerator: sum of available output for the cutoff-≥5 institutions. Measures are mixed: degrees, graduates, enrolment, examined candidates and first-examination passes.
- Denominator: Commission establishment-plan posts (interpolated from the five-year A panel) plus **92** current CJEU judicial positions, held constant.
- Rate: aggregate output per 1,000 positions.
- Data: `data/analysis/gating_cutoff5_output_per_position.csv`.

## Caution

This is **not a valid overproduction measure**. The number of contributing institutions changes from 4 to 13, and the numerator mixes degrees, graduates, enrolment and examination passes. The large movements in the line are therefore composition and coverage effects, not a demonstrated production trend.

For a usable version, the numerator must be a harmonised degree total and the contributing panel must be fixed, or the index must be constructed from institution-level growth rates rather than summed levels.
