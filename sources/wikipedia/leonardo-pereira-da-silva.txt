Afogadense International career 1995 Brazil U20 * Club domestic league appearances and goals&#160;as of 14 October 2023 Leonardo Pereira da Silva (13 June 1974 – 1 March 2016), simply known as Leonardo , was a Brazilian professional footballer who played as a forward . &#91; 1 &#93; &#91; 2 &#93; Career [ edit ] He emerged as champion of Piauí in 1991, at Picos. Negotiated with Sport, he won the Pernambuco championship three times and the 1994 Copa do Nordeste. He was negotiated with Vasco and later with Corinthians and Palmeiras, but he did not take revenge. Upon his return to Sport, he established himself as the club's biggest star, winning the state championship once again three more times. &#91; 3 &#93; In 1995 he was part of the Brazil under-20 team at the Toulon Tournament . &#91; 4 &#93; For Sport Recife he had 367 appearances and scored 133 goals. &#91; 5 &#93; Honours [ edit ] Picos Campeonato Piauiense : 1991 Sport Campeonato Pernambucano : 1992, 1994, 1995, 1998, 1999, 2000 Copa do Nordeste : 1994, 2000 Brazil U20 Toulon Tournament : 1995 Individual Campeonato Pernambucano top scorer: 1997, 1999 Death [ edit ] Leonardo was hospitalized at Restauração Hospital, Recife, on 3 February 2016 after suffering from a seizure. After MRI examinations, a large brain lesion was revealed, caused by neurocysticercosis . On March 1, he died at the age of 41. &#91; 6 &#93; His body was laid to rest at the Sport Recife headquarters. &#91; 7 &#93; References [ edit ] .mw-parser-output
