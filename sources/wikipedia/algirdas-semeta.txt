Biography [ edit ] A native of Vilnius , Algirdas Šemeta graduated in 1985 from Vilnius University 's Faculty of Economic Cybernetics and Finance with a degree as economist-mathematician. &#91; 1 &#93; His previous position has been as Lithuania's Minister of Finance , which he fulfilled from December 2008 to June 2009, having already served in the post a decade earlier, from February 1997 to June 1999. &#91; 2 &#93; Šemeta has minimal business experience in the private sector, his career to date has been concentrated in public office as a civil servant. &#91; 3 &#93; He was European Commissioner for Taxation and Customs Union, Audit and Anti-Fraud from July 2009 till November 2014. &#91; 4 &#93; &#91; 5 &#93; Following his Commission nomination by center-right Prime Minister Andrius Kubilius , Šemeta has affiliated himself with the European People's Party (EPP). &#91; 6 &#93; &#91; 7 &#93; Since December 2014 Šemeta is Business Ombudsman in Ukraine . &#91; 8 &#93;
