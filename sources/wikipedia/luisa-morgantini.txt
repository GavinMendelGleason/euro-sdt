Italian politician (born 1940) 
 
 

 Luisa Morgantini (born 5 November 1940) is an Italian politician who served as the Vice president in the 6th European parliament . She was elected an MEP as an independent with the nomination of the Communist Refoundation Party and sat with The left group in the European parliament .
 She is one of the founders of the Italian branch of the Women in Black organisation.
 
 
 Career [ edit ] 
 Morgantini was the first woman elected to the secretariat of the FLM in Milan ( Federation of Metalworkers &#160;&#91; it &#93; – FIM , FIOM , UILM , representing FIM- CISL ).
 She was elected to the European Parliament in 1999 and re-elected in 2004 as an independent candidate on the list of the Communist Refoundation Party , in the central electoral district, after Fausto Bertinotti opted to take his seat in the southern district. Morgantini received 29,000 preference votes. &#91; 1 &#93; 
 In January 2007, she was elected as the Vice President of the European Parliament , with responsibilities related to European policy on Africa and human rights. She was a member of several parliamentary committees, including the Committee on Development , the Committee on Women's Rights and Gender Equality , the Committee on Constitutional Affairs , and the Subcommittee on Human Rights . &#91; 2 &#93; She was also the chair of the Delegation for relations with the Palestinian Legislative Council as well as member of the Delegation to the Euro-Mediterranean Parliamentary Assembly , and the ACP–EU Joint Parliamentary Assembly . Additionally, she contributed to the election observation working group and was involved in the Peace Initiatives Intergroup . &#91; 3 &#93; 
 She was among the founders of the international network Women in Black against war and violence, and took part in the national coordination of the Association for Peace , a nonviolent movement. She also founded, and currently leads, the association AssoPacePalestina , which advocates for peace and human rights in Palestine . &#91; 4 &#93; 
 
 Morgantini at the International Women In Black meeting at Ulcinj , Montenegro in 1999. 
 Morgantini has received several recognitions, including a peace award from the Israeli branch of Women in Black and the Golden Doves for Peace Prize &#160;&#91; it &#93; from the Disarmament Archive . &#91; 5 &#93; 
 In 2023, Morgantini was one of 1,000 women who were nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize . &#91; 6 &#93; 
 On January 30, 2025, she was arrested along with Il Sole 24 Ore journalist Roberto Bongiorni by the Israeli Defense Forces in the settlement of Kiryat Arba , near Hebron . &#91; 7 &#93; They were accused of entering a military area without authorization and were released a few hours later. &#91; 8 &#93; &#91; 9 &#93; 
 The arrest was criticized by public figures such as Nicola Fratoianni and by the Italian National Press Federation . &#91; 10 &#93;
