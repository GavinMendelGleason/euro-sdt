He is a member of Europe Écologie–The Greens , part of the European Greens . On 20 July 2004 he was re-elected a Member of the European Parliament , and he was elected four times one of its Vice-Presidents . His successor is José Bové . In March 2010 regional election, he is the leader of Europe Écologie–The Greens in Midi-Pyrénées. Onesta defines himself as a European federalist , with strong proclivities for regionalism.
