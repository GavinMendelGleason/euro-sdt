Finnish politician (1946–2017) 

 Outi Marjatta Ojala (28 June 1946, Lappee &#91; 1 &#93; – 16 May 2017 &#91; 2 &#93; ) was a Finnish politician of the Left Alliance . She served as a member of the Parliament of Finland from 1991 to 1996 and from 1999 to 2007, and as a Member of the European Parliament from 1996 to 1999. &#91; 3 &#93; She was President of the Nordic Council in 2002. &#91; 4 &#93; 
 Ojala trained as a nurse and was involved in trade unions and local politics in Helsinki , where she was a city councillor 1989–1996 and 2001–2012. &#91; 5 &#93; 
 
 Positions [ edit ] 
 Chairperson of the National Advisory Board on Romani Affairs 1996–2001
