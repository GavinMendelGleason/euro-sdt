## Atlantic Council

**Type:** Think tank  
**HQ:** Washington DC, USA  
**Founded:** 1961  
**Classification:** Atlanticist ✓ | NATO-adjacent ✓ | US-linked ✓

**Commissioner connections:**
- VdL II (declared): Andrius Kubilius (Board of Advisors, EuroGrowth Initiative 2016–17), Henna Virkkunen (via Atlantic Council of Finland)
- Barroso II / Juncker: Kristalina Georgieva (Distinguished International Leadership Award 2020)

**Key facts:** Funded by US DoD, NATO, Gulf states, and major US defence contractors (Raytheon, Lockheed, Northrop). Annual revenue ~$30m. Self-described mission: "galvanise US leadership and engagement in the world." One of the most significant nodes of the US foreign policy establishment. Its EuroGrowth Initiative specifically targets European economic and security alignment with US interests.
