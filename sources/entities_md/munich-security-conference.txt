## Munich Security Conference

**Type:** Annual conference / forum  
**HQ:** Munich, Germany  
**Founded:** 1963  
**Classification:** Atlanticist ✓ | NATO-adjacent ✓ | US-linked ✓

**Commissioner connections:**
- VdL I/II (declared): Ursula von der Leyen (Advisory Board, to 2019)
- Juncker: Federica Mogherini (Advisory Council member)

**Key facts:** Funded primarily by the German federal government plus defence industry sponsors (Airbus, BAE Systems, Rheinmetall, Boeing, Lockheed Martin). Brings together NATO foreign/defence ministers and heads of state annually. Described by critics as "NATO's annual conference." Its Advisory Board/Council is a key node for European security establishment figures.
