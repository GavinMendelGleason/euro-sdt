## RAND Europe

**Type:** Think tank / contract research  
**HQ:** Cambridge, UK  
**Classification:** Atlanticist ✓ | NATO-adjacent ✓ | US-linked ✓

**Commissioner connections:** Post-Barroso II / Juncker: László Andor (post-mandate engagement)

**Key facts:** European arm of the RAND Corporation, which was founded by the US Air Force in 1948 and remains one of the most significant US national security research organisations. RAND Europe conducts contract research for UK MoD, NATO, EU institutions, and US DoD via the RAND Corporation parent. Its Atlanticism is structural and explicit.
