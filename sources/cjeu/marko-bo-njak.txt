Name: Marko Bošnjak
Position: 

Judge

Biography and career

Born in 1974 in Ljubljana (Slovenia), Mr Marko Bošnjak pursued his university career at the Univerza v Ljubljani (University of Ljubljana, Slovenia), where he first obtained a law degree in 1996, then a Master’s degree in law in 1999, before obtaining his Doctorate in law in 2002. He succeeded in the State law exam in 2002.

His professional career began in 1996 at the Institute of Criminology at the Faculty of Law of that university, where he successively held the positions of research assistant from 1996 to 2002, research fellow from 2002 to 2006, then as a senior research fellow and president of the scientific council from 2006 to 2008. Mr Bošnjak has also been involved in teaching law as an assistant professor at the Faculty of Social Sciences at the Univerza v Ljubljani from 2006 to 2011. Thereafter, from 2011, in that same role, he taught criminal procedure law at the Evropska pravna fakulteta Nove univerze (European Law Faculty of the New University, Slovenia) where, in 2012, he became associate professor, a title he kept until 2022. Since 2025, he has been a member of the European Law Institute Senate and an Honorary Professor of Practice at University College London (UK).

In parallel, Mr Bošnjak was an independent advisor, from 2002 until 2006, then principal advisor, until 2008, to the Ustavno sodišče Republike Slovenije (Constitutional Court of the Republic of Slovenia). Between 2008 and 2016, he joined a Slovenian law firm, first as a trainee lawyer and lawyer and then, from 2012, as an associate. He practised in the area of criminal law and was also head of the constitutional and international law department of the firm.

In 2016, Mr Bošnjak was nominated as a judge at the European Court of Human Rights (ECtHR), where, in 2022, he became Section President and Vice-President. In 2024, he was nominated President of the ECtHR, a position he held until 2025.

Mr Bošnjak is the author of numerous publications, in particular in the areas of criminal law, criminal procedure, human rights, constitutional law, international law and criminology.

Mr Bošnjak has been a judge at the Court of Justice since 16 June 2025.
