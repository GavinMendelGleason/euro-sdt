Biography and career

Born in 1966 in Gothenburg (Sweden), Mr Jörgen Hettne obtained, in 1991, a Master’s degree in law at Lunds universitet (Lund University, Sweden), and, in 2008, a doctorate in European integration law at Stockholms universitet (Stockholm University, Sweden).

He began his professional career as a legal advisor at the Swedish Ministry of Foreign Affairs from 1992 to 1995, then, from 1995 to 2001, he joined the then Court of First Instance of the European Communities as a legal secretary under Judge Pernilla Lindh.

Upon returning to his home country, he devoted himself to the teaching and research of European law at Stockholms universitet from 2001 to 2005, and, as a Senior Researcher in Law, Interim Director, and Deputy Director, at the Svenska institutet för europapolitiska studier (Swedish Institute for European Policy Studies) (SIEPS) from 2005 to 2015. In 2012, he was appointed as Associate Professor of EU Law at Lunds universitet, a position he held from 2015 to 2019 within the Department of Business Law of its School of Economics and Management. Between 2019 and 2025, he led that department, becoming a professor of business law in 2020. From 2018 to 2025, he also held the position of Director of the Centre for European Studies within Lunds universitet. Numerous publications in the fields of European law and business law provide evidence of his research activities.

From 2008 to 2009, as well as in 2010 and 2014, on the initiative of the Swedish Government, he participated in several ad hoc projects intended in particular to ensure the compatibility of Swedish legislation with EU law.

Mr Hettne has been a Judge at the General Court since 16 June 2025.
