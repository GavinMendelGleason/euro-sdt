Name: Niels Fenger
Position: 

Judge

Biography and career

Born in 1968 in Copenhagen, Mr Niels Fenger obtained his law degree in 1992 at the Københavns Universitet (University of Copenhagen, Denmark) and his doctorate in law in 2004 at Aarhus Universitet (Aarhus University, Denmark).

He began his professional career at the Danish Ministry of Justice, where he was Head of Section at the European Union Law Office from 1992 to 1993 and at the Civil Law Office from 1993 to 1994. In 1994, he joined the Court of Justice of the European Union as a legal secretary in the cabinet of Advocate General Michael Bendik Elmer.

On his return to Denmark in 1995, he taught, as a researcher, administrative law and EU law at the Københavns Universitet. Between 1996 and 2000, he returned to the Danish Ministry of Justice as Head of Section at the Constitutional and Administrative Law Office.

In 2001, he joined the Danish Ministry of the Interior as Head of Office where he was responsible for monitoring the legality of municipal decisions relating to trade and the environment. In 2002, he was appointed Director of the legal department of the European Free Trade Association (EFTA) Surveillance Authority, a post he held until 2009, representing that institution before the Court of Justice of the European Union and the EFTA Court.

He was a professor of administrative law at the Københavns Universitet from 2009, a post which he held until 2017. He is the author of several books and numerous articles on EU law, administrative law and the procedural rules of the Danish courts.

From 2014 to 2015, then from 2017 to 2019, he was a judge at the Østre Landsret (High Court of Eastern Denmark).

From 2014 to 2016, he was also a member of the Danish committee responsible for ruling on the admissibility of appeals in civil and criminal matters.

From 2019 to 2024, Mr Fenger held the post of Danish Parliamentary Ombudsman.

Mr Fenger was appointed as a Judge at the Court of Justice on 7 October 2024.

Honorary titles and awards

Ridder af 1. grad af Dannebrogordenen (Knight 1st Class of the Order of Dannebrog (2021))
