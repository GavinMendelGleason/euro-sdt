Supervisory Board
Sebastian Fay.

Sulzbach (Taunus). Supervisory Board Mercedes-Benz Group AG – Employee representative. Member of the Legal Affairs Committee and the Audit Committee. Head of Collective Bargaining Policy and Craft Trades at IG Metall Headquarter Frankfurt.

Appointed until: 2028. Appointed since: 2024. Nationality: German.

Sebastian Fay was born on July 10, 1984 in Bad Soden am Taunus. After graduating from high school, he studied law at the Johann Wolfgang Goethe University in Frankfurt am Main from 2004 to 2010, specializing in Law & Finance. Following his studies, he completed his legal traineeship at the Wiesbaden Regional Court with a focus on employment law.

Sebastian Fay began his trade union career with a one-year trainee course at IG Metall and then became a trade union secretary at the Heilbronn-Neckarsulm branch of IG Metall. In 2016, he moved to the IG Metall district management in Baden-Württemberg, where he was responsible for collective bargaining policy in the metal and electrical industry until 2021. From February 2021 to December 2023, he then worked at IG Metall Mitte district management, where he also took over responsibility for the steel industry in addition to the metal and electrical industry.

Since January 2024, Sebastian Fay has been Head of Collective Bargaining Policy and Crafts at the IG Metall Executive Board.

Sebastian Fay is a member of the following other statutory supervisory boards and comparable supervisory bodies:

Mercedes-Benz AG (Group mandate)
Adecco Personaldienstleistungen GmbH

In addition, Sebastian Fay serves as an honorary judge at the Frankfurt Labor Court and represents IG Metall on various committees of MetallRente GmbH. He is also a member of the board of the Foundation Bildung und Gesundheitshilfe, Berufs- und Lebenshilfe e.V.

Supervisory Board.

The Supervisory Board of Mercedes-Benz Group AG consists of twenty members, of whom ten are elected by the shareholders and ten are representatives of the employees.

The Mercedes-Benz Group AG (former Daimler AG) is one of the world's most successful automotive companies. With Mercedes-Benz AG, the Group offers a wide range of high-end passenger cars and premium vans. Mercedes-Benz Financial Services is another key pillar of the Group and plays a central role in the financial services business.
