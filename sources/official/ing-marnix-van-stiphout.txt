Member Management Board Banking (MBB), chief operations officer and chief transformation officer.

Born in 1970, Dutch nationality.

​​​​​

Profile

Marnix van Stiphout is chief operations officer (COO) and member of the Management Board Banking since September 2021. Marnix is responsible for translating, overseeing and implementing ING’s strategies into a strategy for the operations function.

 Most important other ancillary positions 
None.
Education

Marnix van Stiphout holds a master’s degree in economics from Vrije Universiteit (VU) Amsterdam and a master’s degree in financial economics from London Metropolitan University.

Career
2021-present 1 September 2021 | member Management Board Banking.
2020-2021 COO ING Retail Banking.
2014-2020 COO ING Wholesale Banking.
2013 Head of ING Wholesale Banking Financial Markets (Netherlands).
2012-2013 Head of ING Wholesale Banking Value Chains.
2008-2012 Head of ING Wholesale Banking Value Chain Management Financial Markets.
1998-2008 Several positions ING Wholesale Banking Global Equity Markets.
1996-1998 Investor Relations Consultancy, Johnson & Company.
