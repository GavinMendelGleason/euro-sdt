Supervisory Board Member
André van Broich
André van Broich is a member of the Supervisory Board effective since April 2012, elected until 2027. He ist also Chairman of the Bayer Group Works Council and Chairman of the Bayer European Forum
OUR SUPERVISORY BOARD
Curriculum Vitae
19.06.1970 André van Broich1, born in Cologne, Germany
1988–1991 Vocational training as a chemical laboratory technician at Bayer AG
Since 1988 Member of the German Mining, Chemical and Energy Industrial Union, Voluntary work in various committees
1990–1996 Youth and Trainee Representative
1991–2000 Employment as a chemical laboratory technician (Energy and Environmental Services)
Since 1997 Member of the Works Council
Since 2000 Full-time Member of the Works Council
Since 2005 Member of the Central Works Council and Economics Committee of Bayer AG
Since 2006 Member of the Bayer European Forum
2007–2008 Continuing studies program in change management at Ruhr University Bochum
2010–2024 Chairman of the Works Council of Bayer's site in Dormagen
Since 2017 Chairman of the Bayer Group Works Council
Since 2024 Chairman of the Bayer European Forum
