Supervisory Board Member
Marianne Maehl
Marianne Maehl is a member of the Supervisory Board effective since September 2024, appointed until 2027. She is also Chairwoman of the Works Council of Bayer's site in Frankfurt am Main
OUR SUPERVISORY BOARD
Curriculum Vitae
18.07.1965 Marianne Maehl1, born in Frankfurt am Main
1984-1987 Apprenticeship as a chemical laboratory assistant, Hoechst AG
1987-1991 Chemical laboratory assistant in biochemistry, Hoechst AG
1991-1994 Chemical laboratory assistant in the area of crop protection, Hoechst AG
1994-2000 Chemical laboratory assistant in chemical pesticide research, AgrEvo GmbH
Since 1998 Member of the Mining, Chemical and Energy Industrial Union and honorary member of various trade union committees
2000-2003 Chemical laboratory assistant in chemical research, Aventis CropScience GmbH
2003-2007 Chemical laboratory assistant, Bayer CropScience GmbH
2007-2012 Chemical laboratory assistant in chemical research and biochemistry, Bayer AG
Since 2002 Member of the Works Council of Bayer's site in Frankfurt am Main
Since 2012 Exempted member of the works council
2014-2015 Further education course “Innovation through co-determination” at the Ruhr University Bochum
Since 2017 Member of the Central Works Council and Economic Committee of Bayer AG
2017-2023 Vice Chairwoman of the Works Council of Bayer's site in Frankfurt am Main
2021-2022 Further education course “Participation Management” at Steinbeis University Berlin in cooperation with the Academy of Labor in Frankfurt am Main
Since 2023 Member of the General Works Committee, Bayer Europe Forum and Chairwoman of the Works Council of Bayer's site in Frankfurt am Main
