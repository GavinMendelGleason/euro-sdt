Curriculum Vitae
20.10.1956 Horst Baier2, born in Hannover, Germany
1976–1979 Dual training in business administration (Betriebswirt, VWA), Leibniz Academy and Continental AG, Germany
1979–1990 Different functions (inter alia Head of Foreign Exchange and Cash Management) in the finance department of Continental AG, Germany
1990–1991 Assistant Treasurer at General Tire Inc., USA
1992–1994 Head of Group Controlling, Continental AG, Germany
1994–1996 Director of Group Financing, Schickedanz Group, Germany
1996–2007 Director of Financial Accounting and Reporting, TUI AG, Germany
2007–2016 Member of the Supervisory Board, Hapag-Lloyd AG, Germany
2007–2018 Member of the Board of Directors (since 2012 Chairman) RIU S.A. II, Spain
2007–2018 Member of the Board of Management, responsible for Controlling (as of 2010 Chief Financial Officer), TUI AG, Germany
2009–2014 Member of the Board of Directors (non-executive Director), TUI Travel PLC, United Kingdom
2018-2025 Member of the Supervisory Board, DIAKOVERE gGmbH, Germany
Since 2019 Member of the Supervisory Board, Ecclesia Holding GmbH, Germany
Since 2019 Member of the Board of Directors (non-executive Director), Whitbread PLC, United Kingdom
2 Expert member in the field of accounting pursuant to Section 100, Paragraph 5 of the German Stock Corporation Act (AktG)
