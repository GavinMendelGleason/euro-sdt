CFO and member Executive Board ING Group and member Management Board Banking

Born in 1975, Swedish/Norwegian nationality.

Profile

Ida Lerner is the chief financial officer (CFO) and member of the Executive Board and Management Board Banking since April 2026. Ida is responsible for ING’s financial strategy, budgeting, cost control and the financing of the company.

Most important other ancillary positions
None.
Education

Ida holds a bachelor’s in Social Sciences, with an emphasis on economics from the University of Stockholm.

Career
2026-present 14 April 2026 | CFO and member of the Executive Board.
 1 April 2026 | CFO and member of the Management Board Banking. 
2021-2025 CFO DNB Bank ASA.
2017-2021 Chief risk officier DNB Bank ASA.
2016-2017 General manager DNB Central Europe, Middle East and Africa (CEMEA).
2015-2016 Head of Customer Analysis CEMEA, Large Corporates and International, DNB Sweden.
2012-2014 Head of Customer Analysis Northern Europe, Large Corporates and International, DNB Sweden.
1999-2010 Various relationship management positions across sectors at DNB, HSBC and Nordea in Sweden.
