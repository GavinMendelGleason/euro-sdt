Supervisory Board Member
Alberto Weisser
Alberto Weisser is a member of the Supervisory Board effective since April 2021, elected until 2029. He is also a Senior Consultant at Temasek International Pte. Ltd.

Curriculum Vitae
26.06.1955 Born in São Paulo, Brazil
1977 BA Business Administration, University of São Paulo, Brazil
1978–1993 Various finance-related positions at BASF Group in Brazil, Germany, Mexico and USA

1999–2013 Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and as of 2001 also Chairman of Bunge Limited, USA
