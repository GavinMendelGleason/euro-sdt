SAP SE Extended Board

The SAP SE Extended Board plays a key role in driving SAP’s strategy execution. Its members advise, coordinate, and prepare decisions for the SAP SE Executive Board, drive activities delegated to them and represent SAP externally. Decision power remains with the Executive Board, under unchanged corporate governance.
