Curriculum Vitae
25.12.1967 Yasmin Fahimi1, born in Hannover, Germany
1998 Graduate chemist, Leibniz University of Hannover, Germany
2000–2013 Trade union secretary in various functions at the Mining, Chemical and Energy Industrial Union (IG BCE) in Hanover, Germany
2014–2015 Secretary General of the Social Democratic Party of Germany (SPD)
2016–2017 State Secretary to the German Federal Ministry of Labor and Social Affairs
2017–2022 Member of the German Bundestag
