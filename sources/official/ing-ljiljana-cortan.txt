Member Management Board Banking and head of Wholesale Banking

Born in 1971, Croatian nationality.

Profile

Ljiljana Čortan has been a member of the MBB since 1 January 2021, when she joined ING as chief risk officer (CRO). On 24 February 2026, she became head of Wholesale Banking. As head of ING Wholesale Banking Ljiljana is responsible for the global Wholesale Banking activities.

Most important other ancillary positions
None
Education

Ljiljana holds a master’s degree in business finance from the University of Zagreb.

Career
2026-present 24 February 2026 | Member of Management Board Banking and head of Wholesale Banking
2021-2026 26 April 2021 | CRO and member of the Executive Board.
 1 January 2021 | CRO and member of the Management Board Banking.
2018-2020 Member of the Management Board and CRO, HypoVereinsbank (HVB) - UniCredit Bank, Germany.
2014-2017 Global head of Financial Institutions, Banks and Sovereigns, head of Group Credit Transactions Officer, UniCredit Bank, Italy (Milan branch).
2009-2014 Head of strategy and marketing for corporate and investment banking in the CEE region, UniCredit Bank, Austria.
1996-2009 Various positions in risk management, corporate banking and project/information management at Zagrebačka banka, Croatia (Zagrebačka banka became part of UniCredit) Group in 2002.
