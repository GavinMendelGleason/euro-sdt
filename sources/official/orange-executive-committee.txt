Executive Committee
Executive
Committee
A team leading Orange into the future
Guided by our CEO, Christel Heydemann, the executive committee sets priorities approved by the Board and oversees all key decisions, from business performance and finances to innovation, CSR, and risk management. Made up of 14 members, the committee reflects our commitment to diversity and excellence, supporting 123,500 employees around the world, and working toward a shared vision for all of us.
