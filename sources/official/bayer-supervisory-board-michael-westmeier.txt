Supervisory Board Member
Michael Westmeier
Michael Westmeier is a member of the Supervisory Board effective since April 2022, elected until 2027. He is also Chairman of the Works Council of Bayer Vital GmbH and Vice Chairman of the Bayer Group Works Council
