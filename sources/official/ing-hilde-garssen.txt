Member Management Board Banking (MBB) & chief human resources officer (CHRO)

Born in 1973, Dutch nationality.

Profile

Hilde Garssen is the chief human resources officer (CHRO) and member of the Management Board Banking since September 2026. Hilde is responsible for the people strategy, culture and HR function.

Most important other ancillary positions
None.
Education

Hilde holds a Bachelor’s in Human Resources Management from the HU University of Applied Sciences Utrecht.
