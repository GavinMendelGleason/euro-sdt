LEADERSHIP

Holcim’s Leadership comprises the Executive Committee, which is formally appointed by the Board of Directors. Under the leadership and direction of the CEO, they are responsible for the management of the Group as a dedicated, cohesive and results-driven body.

They take key decisions together, requiring majority votes to pass resolutions, and are committed to accelerating Holcim's growth and delivering on our NextGen Growth 2030 strategy across functions and regions.

CONTACT US

Holcim (SIX: HOLN) has the vision to be the leading partner for sustainable construction. With net sales of CHF 15.7 billion in 2025, we create value across the built environment from infrastructure and industry to buildings. Headquartered in Zug, Switzerland, Holcim has more than 50 000 employees in 45 countries – across Europe, Latin America and Asia, Middle East & Africa – and has been recognized as a Global Top Employer by the Top Employers Institute. Holcim offers high-value end-to-end Building Materials and Building Solutions, from foundation and flooring to walling and roofing – powered by premium brands including ECOPact, ECOPlanet, ECOCycle, and Ytong.
