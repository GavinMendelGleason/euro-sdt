Alfred Stern
Alfred Stern is a member of the Supervisory Board effective since April 2026, elected until 2030. He is also Chief Executive Officer at Irving Oil Ltd.

Memberships in comparable supervising bodies of German or foreign corporations:

Curriculum Vitae
1990 Master of Science, Polymer Engineering and Science, Technical University of Leoben, Austria
1995 Doctoral programme in Mining Sciences, Technical University of Leoben, Austria
1996–2008 Various positions in Research and Development, Marketing and Sales, Quality and Executive Management at E.I. Dupont de Nemours & Company in Switzerland, Germany and the USA, lastly as Global Business Manager Engineering Polymers, USA
2008-2021 Leadership positions in Innovation and Technology and from 2012 in Executive Management, lastly as Chief Executive Officer (CEO), at Borealis AG, Austria
2018 –2023 Member of the Board of the European Chemical Industry Council CEFIC
2021 –2023 Chairman of the Supervisory Board of Borealis AG, Austria
2021 –2026 Executive Board Member for the Chemicals and Materials Division (April to August 2021) and (September 2021 to August 2026) Chairman of the Executive Board and Chief Executive Officer at OMV Aktiengesellschaft, Austria, and President of the Supervisory Board (Consiliul de Supraveghere) of OMV Petrom S.A., Romania
2025 - 2026 Chairman of the Supervisory Board (Aufsichtsrat) of Borealis GmbH (formerly AG), Austria
