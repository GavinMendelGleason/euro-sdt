Governance

Governance is essential for good management and a strategic tool. It ensures not only compliance with regulations, but also the vitality of the business plan, its vision, and its ambitions. According to Danone, governance is based on the implementation of an effective decision-making process, steering with the company's stakeholders, and an evaluation system.

It includes the procedures, rules, and structures that are put in place to ensure transparency in its operations and a balance of power between shareholders, directors, and executives, as well as employees, suppliers, and customers.
