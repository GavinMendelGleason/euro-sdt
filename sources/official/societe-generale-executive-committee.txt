The Executive Committee

The Executive Committee is Societe Generale’s senior management governance body: it assists General Management in the oversight and management of the Group’s activities, operations and functions.

The Societe Generale Executive Committee (EXCO) is a senior executive governance body responsible for the management of Societe Generale and for defining, and managing the full scope of business, operations, and functions of the Group.
