Pedro Azagra
CEO
Iberdrola's Chief Executive Officer
Pedro has a Degree in Law and Business Administration
from the Instituto Católico de Administración y Dirección de Empresas (ICADE) at the Universidad Pontificia de Comillas in Madrid, where he has been imparting his knowledge as professor of Corporate Finance and Mergers and Acquisitions. He was also awarded an MBA from the University of Chicago (United States).
Pedro developed his professional career in the Investment Banking division of Morgan Stanley until joining the Iberdrola Group in 1997 as Director of Strategy. He then took on responsibility for Corporate Development activities until 2022, when he was appointed CEO of Iberdrola's subsidiary in the USA.

José Sainz Armada
CFO
Iberdrola's
Chief Finance, Control and Corporate Development Officer
Graduate in Law and Business Administration
from the Instituto Católico de Administración y Dirección de Empresas (ICADE) at the Universidad Pontificia de Comillas in Madrid, and MBA from INSEAD in Fontainebleau (France).
He previously held positions at J.P. Morgan, Argentaria Bolsa, Banco de Negocios Argentaria, Argentaria and BBVA.
He joined Iberdrola in 2002, and among other positions, he currently serves as chairman of Iberdrola España, S.A.U. and Iberdrola Inmobiliaria, S.A.U., and is a member of the Boards of Directors at Avangrid, Inc., ScottishPower Ltd., and Neoenergia, S.A.

Santiago Martínez Garrido
General Secretary and Secretary of the Board of Directors of Iberdrola S.A.
He holds a law degree from the Universidad Complutense in Madrid,
is a Doctor of Law from the Universidad Autónoma de Barcelona, and has a Diploma in Business Studies from the Centro Universitario San Pablo CEU. He is also a member of the Government’s Legal Service (Cuerpo de Abogados del Estado y Letrados) in the Autonomous Region of Castile and León on voluntary leave.
During his career he has served as Legal Counsel for the Castilla y León regional government in Valladolid, as well as a State Attorney at the High Courts of Justice in Catalonia and Castilla-La Mancha, and at the Interior Ministry. He has served as Chief-of-Staff for the secretary of state for justice and the justice minister, and has served on several Boards of Directors at both public and private companies.
He joined Iberdrola in 2004 and is currently the General Secretary and Secretary of the Board of Directors at Iberdrola, S.A., as well as sitting on the Boards of Directors at Avangrid, Inc. and Neoenergia, S.A.*

Sonsoles Rubio Reinoso
Chief Internal Audit and Risk Officer
She holds a degree in Economics and Business Administration
from the Universidad Autónoma Madrid. She also holds a Master’s Degree in Insurance Management through the ICEA (Cooperative Research Between Insurance Companies and Pension Funds), and underwent the Executive Development Program at the IESE Business School in Navarra. She is a Certified Internal Auditor, Certified Fraud Examiner, Certified Compliance & Ethics Professional, and is Certified in Risk Management Assurance and is a Leading Professional in Ethics & Compliance. She has been the president of the Spanish Institute of Internal Auditors since June 2020.
She has developed her professional career mainly in the field of internal auditing, joining Iberdrola in 2008 as director of Internal Audit at Iberdrola Renovables. She later served as director of Compliance and director of the Internal Audit Area. Since 2023, she has been director of Internal Audit and Risks at Iberdrola.
She has been a board member of Gamesa Corporación Tecnológica, S.A. (later Siemens Gamesa Renewable Energy, S.A.) and of Avangrid. Throughout her career, she has published articles and participated as a speaker at various national and international conferences.
