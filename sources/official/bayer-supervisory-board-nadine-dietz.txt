Nadine Dietz
Nadine Dietz is a member of the Supervisory Board effective since January 2025, elected until 2027. She is also Chairwoman of the Bayer Group Executives' Committee and Vice Chairwoman of the Executives’ Committee at the Leverkusen / Monheim site
