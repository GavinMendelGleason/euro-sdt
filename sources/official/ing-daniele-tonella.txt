Member Management Board Banking (MBB) & chief technology officer

Born in 1971, Swiss nationality.

Profile

Daniele Tonella is chief technology officer (CTO) and member of the Management Board Banking since August 2024. Daniele is responsible for overseeing and managing the total IT landscape and advising on technology-driven business opportunities.

Most important other ancillary positions
Member of the Tech Advisory Board of The Hg Foundation.
Education

Daniele holds an engineering MSc from the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich (ETHZ) and completed various executive education programmes at Harvard, IMD, INSEAD and MIT Sloan.
