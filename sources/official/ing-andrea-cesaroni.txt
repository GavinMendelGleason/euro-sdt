CRO and member Executive Board ING Group and member Management Board Banking

Born in 1971, Italian nationality.

Profile

Andrea Cesaroni is the chief risk officer (CRO) and member of the Executive Board and member of the Management Board Banking since July 2026. Andrea is responsible for the risk frameworks and management of ING Group.

Most important other ancillary positions
None.
Education

Andrea holds a Bachelor’s in Economics from the University of Rome and an MBA from the University of Bologna Business School.
