German Marshall Fund of the United States (GMF) and the Bundeskanzler-Helmut-Schmidt-Stiftung (BKHS) have launched a new Transatlantic Task Force: Together or Alone? Choices and Strategies for Transatlantic Relations for 2021 and Beyond.

Task Force members include:
Rt Hon Baroness Catherine Ashton, Chair of Global Europe, Woodrow Wilson Center; Former EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, and Vice President of the European Commission
