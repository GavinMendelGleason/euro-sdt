Pascal obtained a master’s degree in law from the Université Catholique de 
Louvain, before joining the prestigious College of Europe, where he studied 
European Law. He then was part of the cabinet of the Belgian Minister for 
Internal affairs, before joining the cabinet of the Belgian Chamber of Deputies. 
After working in the private sector as a lawyer specialized in EU matters from 
1988 to 1994, Mr. Leardini joined the European Commission, working as a Desk 
Officer in the DG Internal Market and Services. His work and dedication were 
appreciated as he became Assistant of the same DG. After several positions as 
head of Unit, Pascal Leardini became the Director in charge of the relations 
with the other institutions in the Secretariat -General, Director in charge of 
Institutional and Administrative Policies. He then joined his current position.
