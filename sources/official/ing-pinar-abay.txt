Member Management Board Banking and head of Retail, Market Leaders and Challengers & Growth Markets

Born in 1977, Turkish nationality.

Profile

Pinar Abay is head of Retail, Market Leaders and Challengers & Growth Markets and member of the Management Board Banking since January 2020. Pinar is responsible for defining strategy and priorities for global Retail Banking and driving performance, operations and compliance of Retail, Market Leaders and Challengers & Growth Markets.

Most important other ancillary positions
Non-executive member of the board of ING Belgium N.V./S.A.
Member of the supervisory board of ING-DiBa A.G.
Member of the board of EPI Company SE
Education

Pinar Abay holds a bachelor’s degree in economics from Bilkent University, Turkey, and an MBA degree from Harvard Business School, US.

Career
2023-present 15 May 2023 | member of the supervisory board of ING-DiBa A.G.
 15 May 2023 | head of Retail Banking and head of Challengers & Growth Markets.
2022-2023 Chairwoman of the board of ING in Belgium.
2021-present 22 March 2021 | non-executive member of the board of ING in Belgium.
2020-present 1 January 2020 | member Management Board Banking and head of Market Leaders.
2011 - 2019 Member of the board of ING in Turkey.
1998-2011 Partner and previously various other roles within McKinsey & Company (at McKinsey’s offices in Istanbul, New York and Silicon Valley).
