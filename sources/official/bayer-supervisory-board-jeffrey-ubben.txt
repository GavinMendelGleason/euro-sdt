Supervisory Board Member
Jeffrey Ubben
Jeffrey Ubben is a member of the Supervisory Board effective since April 2024, elected until 2028. He is also Founder, Portfolio Manager and Managing Partner at Inclusive Capital Partners, L.P.
OUR SUPERVISORY BOARD
Memberships in comparable supervising bodies of German or foreign corporations:
Aircela, Inc. (Board of Directors)
Arcadia Power, Inc. (Board of Directors)
Growers Edge Financial, Inc. (Board of Directors)
Noodle Corporation (Board of Directors)
Curriculum Vitae
19.07.1961 Born in Illinois, USA
1983 B.A. in Political Science and Economics from Duke University, USA
1987 M.B.A. from Kellogg School of Management at Northwestern University, USA
1987–1995 Portfolio Manager at Fidelity Value Fund, USA
1995–2000 Managing Partner at Blum Capital Partners, USA
2000–2020 Chief Executive Officer, Chief Investment Officer, and Portfolio Manager at ValueAct Capital, USA
Since 2015 Duke University, Chair of External Engagement (Chair since July 2024), Executive Committee Member
2017-2024 E.O. Wilson Biodiversity Foundation, Member of the Board of Directors, USA
2018-2025 The Redford Center, Member of the Board of Directors, USA
Since 2020 World Wildlife Fund, Member of the Board of Directors, USA
Since 2020 Founder, Portfolio Manager and Managing Partner at Inclusive Capital Partners, L.P., USA
2023–2024 Member of the Sustainability Council of Bayer Aktiengesellschaft, Germany
