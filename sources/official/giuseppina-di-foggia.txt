 
 
 
Giuseppina Di Foggia 
 
Chairman of the Board  
 
Born in Rome in 1969, she has served as Chai rman of the Board of Eni 
SpA since 6 May 2026. 
She is also a member of the Board of Directors of LUISS Business School 
(since May 2022) and of the University of Rome Tor Vergata (since May 
2025). Since 2026, she has been an Academic Fellow at the Department 
of Legal Studies of Bocconi University in Milan, within the “LL.M. in Law of 
Technology and Automated Systems” programme. 
Experience 
In 1998, she joined Lucent Technologies Bell Labs Innovations, a company 
that subsequently became part of Alcatel‑Lucent (from 2006) and later of 
Nokia (from 2016). Over the years, she held a number of senior leadership 
and strategic roles of increasing responsibility, culminating in her 
appointment in April 2020 as Chief Executive Officer and Vice Chairman of 
Nokia Italia, a position she held until May 2023. 
From May 2023 to May 2026, she served as Chief Executive Officer and 
General Manager of Terna SpA. 
She has also held numerous board and advisory roles, including Chairman 
of the Board of Directors of GPI SpA; member of the Executive MBA Board 
at LUMSA University; member of the Advisory Board on Foreign Investors 
of Confindustria; member of the Board of Directors of Cefriel; member of 
the General Council of Anitec Assinform and of the Executive Committee 
of As solombarda; member of the Advisory Boards of Sole 24 Ore 
Formazione, B20 Italy and B7 Italy 2024; as well as Vice Chair man of 
GO15, the global association of Very Large Power Grid Operators for 

electricity transmission. 
She has received  several awards and honours  and in 2021 she was 
appointed Cavaliere del Lavoro della Repubblica Italiana in recognition of 
her contribution to the field of “ Information and Communication 
Technologies”. 
She holds a degree in Electronic Engineering from Sapienza University of 
Rome and is a licensed professional engineer. Her academic background 
also includes a Master’s degree in Professional Program Management from 
the Stevens Institute of Technology (New Jersey, United States), as well 
as executive and general management programmes at SDA Bocconi School 
of Management in Milan and LUISS Business School in Rome.
