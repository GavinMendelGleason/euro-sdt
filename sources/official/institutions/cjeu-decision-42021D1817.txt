 DECISION (EU) 2021/1817 OF THE REPRESENTATIVES OF THE GOVERNMENTS OF THE MEMBER STATES

 of 13 October 2021

 appointing two Judges to the General Court

 THE REPRESENTATIVES OF THE GOVERNMENTS OF THE MEMBER STATES OF THE EUROPEAN UNION,

 Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 19 thereof,

 Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Articles 254 and 255 thereof,

 Whereas:

 (1)

 In accordance with Article 7 of Protocol No 3 on the Statute of the Court of Justice of the European Union, following the appointment of Mr Zoltán CSEHI and Ms Octavia SPINEANU-MATEI to the Court of Justice as Judges, two Judges should be appointed to the General Court for the remainder of the terms of office of Mr Zoltán CSEHI and Ms Octavia SPINEANU-MATEI, which run until 31 August 2022.

 (2)

 Mr Krisztián KECSMÁR and Mr Ion GÂLEA have been nominated for the vacant posts.

 (3)

 The panel set up under Article 255 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union has given a favourable opinion on the suitability of those candidates to perform the duties of Judges of the General Court,

 HAVE ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

 Article 1

 The following persons are hereby appointed to the General Court as Judges for the period from the date of entry into force of this Decision until 31 August 2022:

 —

 Mr Krisztián KECSMÁR,

 —

 Mr Ion GÂLEA.

 Article 2

 This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union .

 Done at Brussels, 13 October 2021.

 The President
