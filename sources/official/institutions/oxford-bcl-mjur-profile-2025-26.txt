                     I am delighted to welcome you to the Oxford Faculty of Law and the BCL and MJur
                     programmes. These are some of the best postgraduate taught programmes in the
                     world, with an illustrious body of alumni working in diverse legal roles across the
                     globe. We are proud to foster a large community of curious, diverse and talented
                     people on the programmes, united by their previous academic achievements and
                     shared enthusiasm for the study of law. I look forward to getting to know you over the
                     year and wish you every success in your studies.

                     Luke Rostill
