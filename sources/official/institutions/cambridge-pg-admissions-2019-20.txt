offers and confirmations

Table 1.1       Applications, Offers and Confirmations by School and Programme Definition (doctoral/non-

                     The total number of applications is 25,346 (7,890 doctoral, 17,456 non-doctoral)

                     The doctoral programmes to non-doctoral programmes application ratio is

                        3,907 (17.4%)
                     Offers: Overseas: 3,809 (52.9% of total offers), Home: 2,279 (31.6%), EU: 1,470 (20.4%)
                     Confirmations: Overseas: 1,951 (48.7% of total confirmations), Home: 1,751 (43.7%),
                        EU: 969 (24.2%)
