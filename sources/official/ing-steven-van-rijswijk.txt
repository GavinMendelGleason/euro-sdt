CEO and chairman Executive Board ING Group and chairman Management Board Banking

Born in 1970, Dutch nationality.

Profile

Steven van Rijswijk is the chief executive officer (CEO) and chairman of the Executive Board and the Management Board Banking since July 2020. Steven is responsible for ING’s strategy, including ESG and sustainability, decision making, results, governance, culture, branding, reputation and people.

Most important other ancillary positions
Member of the Management Board of the Nederlandse Vereniging van Banken (NVB)
Member of the Cyber Security Council (CSR)
Education

Steven van Rijswijk holds a master’s degree in business economics from Erasmus University Rotterdam, the Netherlands.

Career
