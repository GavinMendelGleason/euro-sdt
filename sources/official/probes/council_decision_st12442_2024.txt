Whereas:

(1)    The mandate of the Commission appointed by European Council Decision
       (EU) 2019/19892 comes to an end on 31 October 2024.

(2)    A new Commission, consisting of one national of each Member State, including its
       President and the High Representative of the Union for Foreign Affairs and
       Security Policy, should be appointed until 31 October 2029.

(3)    By Decision (EU) 2024/18623, the European Council proposed
       Ms Ursula VON DER LEYEN to the European Parliament as candidate for President of
       the Commission, and the European Parliament elected her as President of the Commission
       at its Plenary Session of 18 July 2024.

(4)    By Decision (EU) 2024/20864, the European Council, with the agreement of the
       President-elect of the Commission, appointed Ms Kaja KALLAS as High Representative
       of the Union for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy.
