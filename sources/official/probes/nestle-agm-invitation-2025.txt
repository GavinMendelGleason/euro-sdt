2                                           Nestlé I Invitation to the Annual General Meeting 2025
Letter from the Chairman



Dear Shareholder,

After a year of change, we are focusing on our strengths to accelerate our performance
and transformation. Nestlé’s reliable value creation model allows us to invest, innovate and
grow. Our unique, iconic brands and products, an unmatched global presence, leading
innovation and execution capabilities, and our people, position us for continuous value
creation everywhere we operate. Under new leadership, we will assure the disciplined
execution behind our Nutrition, Health and Wellness strategy and reinforce our reputation
as a dependable, reliable company. And we will do so responsibly in a way that creates
shared value for all our stakeholders.

We are making progress on our path to reduce absolute GHG emissions in our supply
chain and to support the transition to a regenerative food system. Last year, for the first
time we submitted our report on non-financial matters to an annual vote by shareholders
as required by Swiss law. This year’s vote will be on our Non-Financial Statement
considering requirements of the European CSRD Regulation. In addition, for the first time
we have issued a voluntary publication, Creating Shared Value at Nestlé, to highlight
particularly relevant actions and accomplishments.

Nestlé’s Board of Directors is highly engaged in overseeing the strategic direction of the
company and taking decisive action when required. Continuous engagement with our
shareholders takes place through our roadshows, investor meetings, analyst calls and
Chairman’s Roundtables, where we meet with investors from different geographies across
the world.

Over the years, we have continued to strengthen the Board by adding new independent
directors with diverse experience and skillsets directly relevant to Nestlé, for example, with
respect to consumer goods, marketing, digitalization, food systems, sustainability,
geopolitics and financial and operational expertise.

This year, the Board proposes to elect Laurent Freixe as member of the Board of Directors.
Laurent Freixe has been with Nestlé for 38 years. He joined Nestlé France in 1986 and
became the Head of the Nutrition Division of Nestlé France in 1999. In 2003, he was
appointed CEO of Nestlé Hungary and in 2007 CEO of the Nestlé Iberian Region. In
November 2008, Laurent joined the Nestlé S.A. Executive Board as Executive Vice
President for Zone Europe and in 2014 was appointed CEO of Zone Americas. In January
2022, he was appointed CEO Zone Latin America. On September 1, 2024, Laurent Freixe
was appointed CEO of the Nestlé Group. Under Nestlé’s governance model, the Board
delegates the operational management of the Company to one of its members
(“administrateur délégué”). This ensures his complete strategic alignment with the Board
and gives the CEO his full authority.
