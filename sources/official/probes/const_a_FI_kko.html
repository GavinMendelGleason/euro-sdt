Supreme Court is the fi­nal in­stance in civil, com­mer­cial and crim­i­nal mat­ters

The Supreme Court exercises judicial power as the final instance in civil and criminal matters and supervises the judicial procedure within its field of competence. The most important function of the Supreme Court is to establish judicial precedents.
