Roche Holding Ltd, Basel, Switzerland

2021 Annual General Meeting
of Shareholders
In view of the continued extremely tense situation in the fight against the coronavirus, our top priority is to protect our shareholders against a possible
health-related threat or infection with COVID-19.
As a precautionary and prudent measure, the Board of Directors has decided to conduct the Annual General Meeting of Roche Holding Ltd
on Tuesday, 16 March 2021, in accordance with Art. 8 of the Federal Act on the Statutory Principles for Federal Council Ordinances on Combating
the COVID-19 Epidemic (COVID-19 Act) and Art. 27 of the Ordinance 3 on Measures to Combat the Coronavirus (COVID-19) (COVID-19 Ordinance 3)
­without the physical presence of shareholders.
The Annual General Meeting will therefore be held on 16 March 2021 at 10.30 am, with only the persons in attendance required by the
­A rticles of Incorporation, at the premises of Roche Holding Ltd, Grenzacherstrasse 124, 4058 Basel.
Roche shareholders may exercise their rights exclusively via written or electronically submitted instructions to the independent proxy,
Testaris AG. The outcome of the vote and election determined on the basis of the instructions to Testaris AG will be published in the minutes at
https://www.roche.com/about/governance/annual_general_meetings.htm.
Further information is available at https://www.roche.com/about/governance/annual_general_meetings.htm.

Authorisation
Shareholders may exercise their rights exclusively via written or
electronically submitted instructions to the independent proxy,
Testaris AG. Under the Company’s Articles of Incorporation, ­shareholders
wishing to exercise their rights may request their voting ­documents
no later than Thursday, 11 March 2021, either from our Company
(phone: +41 (0)55 617 37 32, fax: +41 (0)55 617 37 28) or from any bank
branch domiciled in Switzerland. Shares (valor number: 1,203,211) must
be deposited with the issuer. § 12 of the Articles of Incorporation requires
that the voting documents be issued in shareholders’ names. Shareholders
depositing shares with a bank are to request that the bank informs the
Company of the names of the shareholders to whom the voting documents
have been issued.

Appointment of independent proxy
Testaris AG, Malzgasse 18, 4052 Basel, Switzerland, serves as the
designated independent proxy as defined in Art. 689c of the Swiss
Code of Obligations and Art. 8 of the Ordinance against excessive
compensation at listed joint-stock companies (VegüV). Shareholders
wishing to be represented by the independent proxy should send their
proxy authorisations and instructions in writing or using the electronic
authorisations and instructions to the independent proxy.

Electronic authorisations and instructions
to the independent proxy
Shareholders may cast their votes remotely at
https://www.roche.com/about/governance/annual_general_meetings.htm
by issuing electronic authorisations and instructions to the independent
proxy. The requisite login data is enclosed with the meeting materials
supplied to shareholders. Shareholders thus have the option of voting
remotely by issuing electronic authorisations and instructions to the
independent proxy. Shareholders may vote by remote electronic ballot,
or change any instructions they may have communicated electronically,
up to but no later than Sunday, 14 March 2021, at 8.00 pm.
