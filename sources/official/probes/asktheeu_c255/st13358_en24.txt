    I.   Appointment of members of the Court of Justice

          1.   In a letter dated 5 April 20231, the President of the Court of Justice informed the
               President of the Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of the Member
               States (IGC) that the term of office of 13 Judges and five Advocates-General of the
               Court of Justice would end on 6 October 2024.

          2.   That being the case:

               -   the Government of the Republic of Austria proposed that Mr Andreas Kumin2 be
                   reappointed as a Judge of the Court of Justice;

      3.    Following the resignation, with effect from 7 October 20245, of the Judge of the Court
            of Justice of the European Union appointed on a proposal from the government of the
            Kingdom of Denmark, it was for the government of that country to propose a
            candidate for the post of Judge of the Court of Justice for the remainder of the current
            term of office, ending on 6 October 2027. That being the case, the government of the
            Kingdom of Denmark proposed that Mr Niels Fenger6 be appointed as a Judge of the
            Court of Justice.

      4.    Pursuant to the first paragraph of Article 253 in conjunction with the first paragraph of
            Article 255 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, the Judges and
            the Advocates-General of the Court of Justice are chosen from persons whose
            independence is beyond doubt and who possess the qualifications required for
            appointment to the highest judicial offices in their respective countries or who are
            jurisconsults of recognised competence; they are appointed by common accord of the
            governments of the Member States, after consultation of a panel set up in order to give
            an opinion on candidates’ suitability to perform the duties of Judge or Advocate-
            General of the Court of Justice.

     II.    Appointment of members of the General Court

       6.    In a letter dated 26 March 20218, the President of the General Court informed the
             President of the Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of the Member
             States that the terms of office of 26 Judges would end on 31 August 2022.

       7.    That being the case, the government of the Slovak Republic nominated
             Mr Juraj Schwarcz9 for a renewal of his term of office.

             The proposed appointment is for a term of office ending on 31 August 2028.

       8.    In addition, in a letter dated 29 February 202410, the President of the General Court
             informed the President of the Conference of the Representatives of the Governments
             of the Member States that the term of office of 26 Judges would end on
             31 August 2025.

       9.    That being the case, the government of Ireland nominated Ms Suzanne Kingston and
             Mr Colm Mac Eochaidh, and the government of the Kingdom of Spain nominated
             Mr José Martín y Pérez de Nanclares and Mr Miguel Sampol Pucurull, for a renewal
             of their terms of office11.

      10.   In addition, a Judge of the General Court, appointed on a proposal from the French
            Republic, was appointed as a Judge of the Court of Justice for the period from

            of Judge of the General Court for the remainder of the term of office of the outgoing
            Judge, i.e. until 31 August 2025. That being the case, the French Republic nominated
            Mr Hervé Cassagnabère13 for the post of Judge of the General Court.

      11.   Lastly, a Judge of the General Court, appointed on a proposal from the government of
            the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, was appointed Advocate-General to the Court of
            Justice for the period from 7 October 202414. It was therefore for that country to
            propose a candidate for the post of Judge of the General Court for the remainder of the
            term of office of the outgoing Judge, i.e. until 31 August 2028. That being the case,
            the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg nominated Mr Raphaël Meyer 15 for the post of
            Judge of the General Court.

      12.   Pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 254 in conjunction with the first paragraph
            of Article 255 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, members of

            who possess the ability required for appointment to high judicial office; they are
            appointed by common accord of the governments of the Member States, after
            consultation of a panel set up in order to give an opinion on candidates’ suitability to
            perform the duties of Judge of the General Court.

    III.     Convening of a Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of the Member
             States

       14.    In the light of the above, a Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of
              the Member States (IGC) will be held in the margins of the Coreper meeting on
              2 October 2024 to examine the opinions given by the panel provided for in Article 255
              of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and take the relevant
              decisions. The minutes of the previous IGC, held on 3 July 2024, will also be
              submitted to that IGC for approval.
