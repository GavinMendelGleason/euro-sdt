THE REPRESENTATIVES OF THE GOVERNMENTS OF THE MEMBER STATES OF THE
EUROPEAN UNION,

Having regard to the Treaty on European Union, and in particular Article 19 thereof,

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular
Articles 254 and 255 thereof,

                                               JUR.4             LIMITE                   EN
Whereas:

(1)    The terms of office of 26 Judges of the General Court expired on 31 August 2025.

(2)    In that context, it has been proposed that the term of office of Mr Eugène BUTTIGIEG as
       Judge of the General Court be renewed.

(3)    The panel set up under Article 255 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union
       has given a favourable opinion on the suitability of this candidate to perform the duties of
       Judge of the General Court.

(4)    Mr Eugène BUTTIGIEG should therefore be appointed for the period from the date of
       entry into force of this Decision to 31 August 2031,

HAVE ADOPTED THIS DECISION:




29505/26                                                                                              2
                                              JUR.4              LIMITE                         EN
                                                Article 1

Mr Eugène BUTTIGIEG is hereby appointed Judge to the General Court for the period from the
date of entry into force of this Decision to 31 August 2031.


                                                Article 2

This Decision shall enter into force on the date of its publication in the Official Journal of the
European Union.
