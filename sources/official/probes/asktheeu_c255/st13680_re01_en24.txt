I.   Appointment of members of the Court of Justice

     1.      In a letter dated 5 April 2023 1, the President of the Court of Justice informed the
             President of the Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of the Member
             States (IGC) that the term of office of 13 Judges and 5 Advocates-General of the Court
             of Justice would end on 6 October 2024.

        2.   That being the case:

             -   the Government of the Republic of Austria proposed that Mr Andreas Kumin 2 be
                 reappointed as a Judge of the Court of Justice;

             -   the Government of the Portuguese Republic nominated DELETED 3 for a first term
                 of office as a Judge of the Court of Justice;

      3.   Following the resignation, with effect from 7 October 2024 6, of the Judge of the Court
           of Justice of the European Union appointed on a proposal from the Government of the
           Kingdom of Denmark, it was for the government of that country to propose a candidate
           for the post of Judge of the Court of Justice for the remainder of the current term of
           office, ending on 6 October 2027. That being the case, the Government of the Kingdom
           of Denmark nominated Mr Niels Fenger 7 for the post of Judge of the Court of Justice.

      4.   Pursuant to the first paragraph of Article 253 in conjunction with the first paragraph of
           Article 255 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, the Judges and the
           Advocates-General of the Court of Justice are chosen from persons whose independence
           is beyond doubt and who possess the qualifications required for appointment to the
           highest judicial offices in their respective countries or who are jurisconsults of
           recognised competence; they are appointed by common accord of the governments of
           the Member States, after consultation of a panel set up in order to give an opinion on
           candidates’ suitability to perform the duties of Judge or Advocate-General of the Court
           of Justice.

II.   Appointment of members of the General Court

       6.   In a letter dated 26 March 2021 9, the President of the General Court informed the
            President of the Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of the Member
            States that the terms of office of 26 Judges would end on 31 August 2022.

       7.   That being the case, the Government of the Slovak Republic nominated
            Mr Juraj Schwarcz 10 for a renewal of his term of office.

            The proposed appointment is for a term of office ending on 31 August 2028.

       8.   In addition, in a letter dated 29 February 2024 11, the President of the General Court
            informed the President of the Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of
            the Member States that the term of office of 26 Judges would end on 31 August 2025.

       9.   That being the case, the Government of Ireland nominated Ms Suzanne Kingston and
            Mr Colm Mac Eochaidh, and the Government of the Kingdom of Spain nominated
            Mr José Martín y Pérez de Nanclares and Mr Miguel Sampol Pucurull, for a renewal of
            their terms of office 12.

      10.   In addition, a Judge of the General Court, appointed on a proposal from the French
            Republic, was appointed as a Judge of the Court of Justice for the period from
            7 October 2024 13. It was therefore for that country to propose a candidate for the post
            of Judge of the General Court for the remainder of the term of office of the outgoing
            Judge, i.e. until 31 August 2025. That being the case, the French Republic nominated
            Mr Hervé Cassagnabère 14 for the post of Judge of the General Court.

      11.   Lastly, a Judge of the General Court, appointed on a proposal from the Government of
            the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg, was appointed as an Advocate-General of the Court
            of Justice for the period from 7 October 2024 15. It was therefore for that country to
            propose a candidate for the post of Judge of the General Court for the remainder of the
            term of office of the outgoing Judge, i.e. until 31 August 2025. That being the case, the
            Grand Duchy of Luxembourg nominated Mr Raphaël Meyer 16 for the post of Judge of
            the General Court.

      12.   Pursuant to the second paragraph of Article 254 in conjunction with the first paragraph
            of Article 255 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, members of the
            General Court are chosen from persons whose independence is beyond doubt and who
            possess the ability required for appointment to high judicial office; they are appointed
            by common accord of the governments of the Member States, after consultation of a
            panel set up in order to give an opinion on candidates’ suitability to perform the duties
            of Judge of the General Court.

III. Meeting of the Conference of the Representatives of the Governments of the Member
     States

     17.    By an information note 17 from the Secretariat of the Conference of the Representatives
            of the Governments of the Member States (IGC), the Permanent Representatives of the
            Member States were informed of the convening of an IGC, in the margins of the Coreper
            meeting on 2 October 2024, with a view to examining those opinions and taking the
            appropriate decisions.

     18.    Accordingly, the IGC could, at its meeting on 2 October 2024, adopt the decisions
            appointing Mr Andreas Kumin, DELETED and Mr Niels Fenger as Judges of the Court
            of Justice, and the decision appointing Mr Andrea Biondi as Advocate-General of the
            Court of Justice, as set out in 13841/24, 13810/24, 13842/24 and 13843/24. The IGC
            could also, at its meeting on 2 October 2024, adopt the decision appointing
            Mr Juraj Schwarcz, Ms Suzanne Kingston, Mr Colm Mac Eochaidh, Mr José Martín y
            Pérez   de   Nanclares,   Mr Miguel Sampol Pucurull,     Mr Hervé Cassagnabère      and
            Mr Raphaël Meyer as Judges of the General Court, as set out in 12659/24.

     19.    The IGC could also approve the minutes of the previous IGC, which took place on
            3 July 2024, as set out in 12064/1/24.
