# Council of the EU — appointment decisions relevant to batch B, 2021–2026

Text extracted verbatim from the Council press releases below (the live pages return a
browser-verification page to non-browser clients; archived captures were read where the live
page was blocked). These establish **which seats were open** and **who was appointed**, i.e. the
denominator for any national candidate pool.

## 2021-02-19 — five judges and an advocate-general appointed
URL: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2021/02/19/eu-court-of-justice-five-judges-and-an-advocate-general-appointed/
(archived capture: https://web.archive.org/web/20240509133300/)

> The nominations are part of the partial renewal of the composition of the Court of Justice,
> since the terms of office of 14 judges and 6 advocates-general will expire on 6 October 2021.
> Mr Miroslav Gavalec (Slovakia) and Ms Octavia Spineanu-Matei (Romania) have been appointed
> judges to the Court of Justice for a first term of office.
> The terms of office of Mr Niilo Jääskinen (Finland) and Mr Lars Bay Larsen (Denmark) as judges
> of the Court of Justice have been renewed.
> ...
> The four judges and the advocate-general of the Court of Justice have been appointed for a term
> of office starting on 7 October 2021 and expiring on 6 October 2027.

## 2021-12-21 — nine judges of the General Court appointed
URL: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2021/12/21/eu-court-of-justice-nine-judges-of-the-general-court-appointed/

> (Cyprus) and Ms Tuula Pynnä and Mr Heikki Kanninen (Finland) were reappointed as judges of the

## 2023-09-15 — two judges appointed to the General Court
URL: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2023/09/15/eu-court-of-justice-two-judges-appointed-to-the-general-court/

> Mr Saulius Lukas Kalėda was appointed as a judge at the General Court for a term of office
> ending on 31 August 2025. His appointment was made as part of the partial renewal of the
> composition of the General Court in 2019.

> Nielsen from this post.

## 2023-11-08 — two judges and an advocate-general reappointed
URL: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2023/11/08/eu-court-of-justice-two-judges-and-an-advocate-general-reappointed/

> The representatives of the governments of the member states today reappointed Mr Constantinos
> Lycourgos and Mr Jan Passer as judges to the Court of Justice.
> Mr Giovanni Pitruzzella was reappointed as advocate-general to the Court of Justice.
> The three appointments are for a term of office starting on 7 October 2024 and ending on
> 6 October 2030.

## 2024-07-03 — three judges and an advocate-general appointed

## 2025-03-26 — thirteen judges to the General Court
URL: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/03/26/court-of-justice-of-the-european-union-member-states-representatives-appoint-thirteen-judges-to-the-general-court/

> The terms of office of Mr Hervé Cassagnabère (France), Mr Saulius Lukas Kalėda (Lithuania),
> Mr Ulf Öberg (Sweden), Ms Tamara Perišin (Croatia), Mr David Petrlík (Czechia), Ms Inga Reine
> (Latvia), Ms Petra Škvařilová-Pelzl (Czechia), Mr Laurent Truchot (France) and Mr Pēteris
> Zilgalvis (Latvia) have been renewed.

> Moreover, Ms Danute Jočienė (Lithuania) has been appointed judge to the General Court as a
> replacement for Mr Rimvydas Norkus ... for the remainder of his term of office, which runs
> until 31 August 2025.
> Finally, Mr Jörgen Hettne (Sweden) has been appointed judge to the General Court as a
> replacement for Mr Fredrik Schalin ... until 31 August 2025.

## 2026-09-30 — reappointment of Niels Fenger (context for the Danish seat)
