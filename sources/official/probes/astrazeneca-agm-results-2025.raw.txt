Results of Annual General Meeting held on 11 April 2025

AstraZeneca PLC announced the results of the voting at its Annual General Meeting (AGM)
today. As proposed in the Notice of AGM, all Resolutions were decided by poll vote.
Resolutions 9 – 12 were passed as special resolutions; all other resolutions were passed as
ordinary resolutions.

Issued capital
As at 9 April 2025, the number of issued shares of the Company was 1,550,636,615 ordinary
shares, which was the total number of shares entitling the holders to attend and vote for or
against all of the resolutions at the AGM. In accordance with the Company's Articles of
Association, on a poll every member present in person or by proxy has one vote for every
share held.
AstraZeneca
AstraZeneca (LSE/STO/Nasdaq: AZN) is a global, science-led biopharmaceutical company
that focuses on the discovery, development, and commercialisation of prescription medicines
in Oncology, Rare Diseases, and BioPharmaceuticals, including Cardiovascular, Renal &
Metabolism, and Respiratory & Immunology. Based in Cambridge, UK, AstraZeneca's
innovative medicines are sold in more than 125 countries and used by millions of patients
worldwide. Please visit astrazeneca.com and follow the Company on social
media @AstraZeneca.
