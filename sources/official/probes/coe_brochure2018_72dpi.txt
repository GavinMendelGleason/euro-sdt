PROMOTION SIMONE VEIL



    Biographical note
 Simone Veil (1927-2017) served as the first president of the directly-elected European Parliament
 and as such the first woman at the head of any EU institution. Through her indefatigable and
 profoundly human engagement she has left her mark on the European construction, the evolution
 of the rights of women in Europe and the combat against anti-Semitism.


   Note bibliographique

 sel et en tant que tel la première femme à la tête d’une institution européenne. Elle a marqué par
 son engagement infatigable et profondément humain la construction européenne, l’évolution des
 droits des femmes en Europe et la lutte contre l’antisémitisme.
