Discrepancy: this table gives 494 for 2024-2025, while the Rector's
2020-2025 mandate report gives 496 for 2024-25. The factsheet for 2024
gives 340 + 120 + 32 = 492. The three official College figures for the
same year differ; none of them is a graduate count.
