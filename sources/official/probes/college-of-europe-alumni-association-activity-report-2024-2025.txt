    1. WORD OF THE PRESIDENT


Dear Fellow Alumni,

Happy New Year. I wish you a wonderful, healthy, and successful 2026 ahead!

Thank you for the positive feedback and the increasing engagement across our community, which I
hope reflect the progress made over the past two years. At the same time, I am convinced that our
Association has even greater potential and that, together, we can do more - and do it better.

Today, nearly 3,000 alumni actively participate in our events and activities, and more than 9, 000
alumni are connected through the Hivebrite platform. Our Anniversary Dinners bring together
generations of alumni in a spirit of continuity and belonging, while initiatives such as the EPSO
training and Mentorship Programme support young professionals at critical stages of their careers.
Through the Alumni Lunch Series, we have also fostered in-depth exchanges with EU leaders,
offering alumni the opportunity to engage directly with top officials and experts on Europe’s future.
Our regional groups are becoming more engaged, helping to connect alumni across the globe.

Looking ahead, my priority is to launch the process of institutionalising the Alumni
Association within the College of Europe. This reflection is driven by a simple conviction: our
Association can achieve greater impact, continuity, and ambition if its work is supported by a more
sustainable structure. As a volunteer-led organisation supported by a single administrative officer,
our resources are necessarily limited.

I would like to extend my sincere gratitude to the Administrative Officer and the Board of the Alumni
Association for their dedication and commitment. Above all, I thank you - our Alumni - whose ideas,
participation, and willingness to give back are the true strength of this community.

Finally, I would like to express my gratitude to the College of Europe for its continued trust, support,
and close cooperation. Together, we strive to ensure that L’Esprit du Collège remains alive -
contributing, in our own way, to the Europe of today and tomorrow!

With my warmest regards,

Dr. Teona Lavrelashvili
President, College of Europe Alumni Association

Alumni Lunch Series

The Alumni Lunch Series has established itself as one of our most
successful initiatives. Established in 2025, these informal, off-
the-record exchanges between senior alumni and distinguished
guests from the EU institutional sphere provide a unique forum for
in-depth dialogue on key developments and institutional
perspectives, held under the Chatham House Rule. The series has
also become a highly valued networking and learning platform,
fostering meaningful connections and knowledge exchange
across generations of alumni.

   6. MEMBERSHIP

The membership revenue continued to grow steadily over the year, reaching over €15,500 by
September 2025, supported by 439 active memberships. Purchases increased by 66% compared
to the previous year (see graph below), reflecting strong engagement from alumni. This growth
coincides with targeted campaigns and flagship initiatives, such as the Mentorship Programme and
the EPSO AD5 Competition Training offer, which have not only provided valuable opportunities for
professional development but also encouraged alumni to actively invest in their membership. The
increase in subscriptions demonstrates the continued relevance of the Association’s activities and
the value that alumni place on being connected to the wider College network.

   7. PARTNERSHIPS

The Alumni Association continues to develop and strengthen strategic partnerships to provide
alumni with enhanced professional and personal opportunities.

   v UK Mission to the EU: Collaboration on the 2025/26 Leadership Event Series, building on
       last year’s successful initiative. Alumni will participate in high-level discussions on
       economic development, innovation, and security, while reinforcing the Association’s
       connections with key EU stakeholders.
   v   Aspria: Offers alumni reduced membership rates and special conditions for existing
       members, supporting well-being, networking, and access to premium facilities.
   v   Alliance Française: Provides discounted and tailored French courses, covering general,
       legal, diplomatic, EU affairs, and business French. This initiative enhances professional
       development and supports personal and cultural growth within the community.
   v   EU Training: The Association has established a partnership with EU Training, which provides
       alumni with discount codes to access online materials and webinars for EPSO preparation,
       including support for AD5 competitions. Alumni benefit from practical exam preparation,
       personal coaching, and tailored workshops, helping them strengthen their skills and
       succeed in EU career opportunities.
   v   What’s Up EU: A weekly newsletter summarising key EU developments. They have offered
       to draft minutes of gatherings and conferences organised by the Alumni Association, which
       alumni can access through our channels. Alumni benefit from concise, reliable updates on
       EU affairs and enhanced engagement with the broader European community.

Overall, these strategic partnerships serve to broaden opportunities for alumni, strengthen our
network, and enhance the value of membership in the College of Europe Alumni Association.
