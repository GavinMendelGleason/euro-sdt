Heading on the page: "The College in numbers (2024):"

Verbatim items relevant to production:

- 340 students in Bruges (Belgium)
- 120 students in Natolin (Warsaw, Poland)
- 32 students in Tirana (Albania)
- 17.000+ alumni
- 7 Masters programmes
- 70% of our students are funded through scholarships
- 50 to 60 nationalities represented across the three campuses
- Average age of students: 25.3 years

Observations:
- No annual graduate count is given anywhere on the factsheet.
- "17.000+ alumni" is a cumulative stock since 1949, printed as a floor, and
  is not an annual flow.
- The campus figures sum to 492, which differs from 494 (promotions table)
  and 496 (Rector's 2020-2025 mandate report) for 2024-25.
