Results of Annual General Meeting held on 27 April 2023

AstraZeneca PLC announced the results of the voting at its Annual General Meeting (AGM)
today. As proposed in the Notice of AGM, all Resolutions were decided by poll vote.
Resolutions 9 - 13 were passed as special resolutions; all other resolutions were passed as
ordinary resolutions.

AstraZeneca
AstraZeneca (LSE/STO/Nasdaq: AZN) is a global, science-led biopharmaceutical company
that focuses on the discovery, development, and commercialisation of prescription medicines
in Oncology, Rare Diseases, and BioPharmaceuticals, including Cardiovascular, Renal &
Metabolism, and Respiratory & Immunology. Based in Cambridge, UK, AstraZeneca operates
in over 100 countries and its innovative medicines are used by millions of patients worldwide.
