Accessibility notice (in Estonian)

Text size

In all popular web browsers, you can zoom in and out by holding the Ctrl key (Cmd key on macOS) and pressing either the "+" or "-" key. Another convenient option is to use the mouse: hold the Ctrl key and scroll the mouse wheel. To return to normal size, press Ctrl and 0 simultaneously.

Villu Kõve: AI should not replace justices, but empower them

President of the Supreme Court Villu Kõve and his adviser Karin Leichter-Tammisto attended the international conference “On the Constitution, the State and Democracy” in Tivat, Montenegro from October 1 to 4, 2026.

02 October 2026

Ukrainian Constitutional Court learned about Estonian legal system

The Supreme Court of Estonia hosted a delegation from the Constitutional Court of Ukraine between September 20 and 25 to introduce Estonia’s system of constitutional review, ongoing judicial reform and the digitalisation of the administration of justice, and to exchange experiences on the organisation of the work of the courts in the two countries.
News
11 September 2026

Delegation of the Supreme Court visited the CJEU

On September 7th and 8th, a delegation of the Supreme Court visited the Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU) in Luxembourg. The visit included discussions on various legal issues, ranging from preliminary rulings to data protection in administrative and criminal proceedings.
